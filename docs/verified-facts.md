# Verified facts used in website copy

The live site (hytengineering.com) could not be reached from the build
environment, so facts were taken from public sources that quote HYT's own
material. **Every item below must be confirmed by HYT before launch.** Items
marked ⚠ are reported inconsistently between sources.

## Company
* Legal name: HYT Engineering Company Pvt. Ltd. (CIN U29299PN1989PTC052195, incorporated 14 June 1989, RoC Pune).
* Origins: began in **1977** (as HYT Engineering Corporation) building small drilling machines and lathes; describes itself as India's pioneer in CNC wheel lathe technology since 1977.
* Registered office / works: 29/10, 29/10/1, 29/10/2, D-II Block, MIDC Chinchwad, Pune – 411019, Maharashtra, India.
* Telephone: +91 20 66343900 / 06. Toll-free: 1800 2100 456. Email: info@hytengineering.com. Hours: Monday–Saturday 9:30–18:30, Sunday closed.
* A New Delhi sales office is mentioned on an older contact page (address not verified – not published).
* Certifications: ISO 9001:2015, ISO 14001:2015, ISO 45001:2018.
* ⚠ Machines delivered: "1,100+ machines delivered worldwide" (current site), "over 500 machines" (exhibitor profile), "928 machines till June 2022" (older page). The site uses **1,100+** pending confirmation.
* ⚠ Installed base of underfloor + tandem underfloor lathes: 257 or 262 units (two sources). Not used in copy.
* Listed in Dun & Bradstreet "Leading Mid-Corporates of India 2023" (publication listing – confirm).
* Exhibitor at Asia Pacific Rail, Middle East Rail, Africa Rail 2020 and AusRAIL (Terrapinn / AusRAIL exhibitor listings).

## Customers and projects (public sources)
* Indian Railways – contract to supply nine CNC underfloor wheel lathes (Railway Gazette).
* National Capital Region Transport Corporation (NCRTC) – Letter of Acceptance for fully automatic CNC underfloor wheel lathes.
* Delhi Metro Rail Corporation (DMRC) – award for a new CNC tandem underfloor wheel lathe (HYT home page).
* State Railway of Thailand (Bangkok) – CNC vertical turret lathe supplied and installed.
* Sanpada EMU car shed, Mumbai – CNC vertical turning lathe supplied (HYT executed-projects page).

## Product data (HYT catalogue pages)
* **YUL-08 CNC Underfloor Wheel Lathe** – main drive 4 × 15 kW. Re-profiles both wheels of a wheelset simultaneously without removing the wheelset or brake gear.
* **YUL-12T CNC Tandem Underfloor Wheel Lathe** – two machines running in synchronised tandem; one fixed, one traversing to suit bogie wheelbase; main drive 4 × 15 kW; tread diameter 540–1500 mm; cutting speed 20–120 m/min; re-profiling time reduced by 50 % (coaches) and 33 % (locomotives) versus a single machine (company claim).
* **YPL-02 CNC Portal Wheel Lathe** – tread Ø max 1250 mm / min 750 mm*; wheel width 75–145 mm; axle length 1850–2750 mm*; max wheelset weight 50 kN; main drive 2 × 55 kW; chip cross-section 32–50 mm². (*depends on gauge)
* **YSL-08 CNC Surface Wheel Lathe** – tread Ø max 1250 mm, ⚠ min 750 mm (HYT) or 540 mm (reseller); wheel width 75–145 mm; axle length 1200–2750 mm*; max wheelset weight 50 kN; brake-disc machining Ø 250–700 mm; main drive 2 × 45 or 55 kW; cutting speed 25–80 m/min. Models YSL-08, YSL-12, YSL-21 exist (catalogue PDFs).
* **YVM-04** – CNC vertical turning & boring machine model code.
* All other products: no verified figures – spec tables show "On request".
