Services

After-sales service network

One number, one service desk and engineers who know your machine. Here is how to reach us and what happens after you call.

Content pending HYT approval: HYT to supply the list of service centres and field service locations (city, address, contact) for publication on this page.

How service is organised

Coordinated from Pune, delivered on site

Every service request is logged and coordinated by our service desk at the HYT works in Pune, where our machines are designed and built. The desk has access to each machine's build records, drawings and service history, so the first conversation is already an informed one.

Field service engineers travel to your site for installation, preventive maintenance, breakdowns and repairs. Where a machine supports remote connection, our engineers can review alarms and parameters before travelling, which often shortens diagnosis and ensures the right parts go with the engineer.

Spare parts are dispatched from Pune. For urgent cases, tell us that the machine is stopped and we will prioritise accordingly.

When you call

What happens after you raise a request

  1. 01

    Call or email

    Use the toll-free line or info@hytengineering.com. Quote the machine model and serial number.

  2. 02

    Logged and assigned

    The service desk records the request and assigns an engineer.

  3. 03

    First diagnosis

    The engineer discusses symptoms and alarms with your team, remotely where possible.

  4. 04

    Action

    Advice by phone, parts dispatch or an engineer visit – whichever solves the problem fastest.

  5. 05

    Closure

    You receive a report and the request is closed once you confirm the machine is running.

Have this ready when you contact us

  • Machine model and serial number
  • Site name and location
  • Name and phone number of a contact on site
  • Description of the problem and any alarm messages
  • Whether the machine is stopped
  • Your contract type, if you have an AMC, CMC or other agreement
