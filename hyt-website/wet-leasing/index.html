Services

Machine as a Service (MAAS): wet leasing of wheel lathes

HYT provides the machine, the people who run it and the maintenance that keeps it running. You receive re-profiled wheelsets without the capital investment or the need to build a specialist team.

The idea

Pay for re-profiled wheels, not for a machine

Buying a wheel lathe means capital approval, a procurement process, civil works, recruitment and training – before a single wheel is machined. For some operators that is the right path. For others, especially those facing an urgent capacity gap or uncertain long-term demand, it is not.

With Machine as a Service, HYT supplies a wheel lathe together with trained operators and full maintenance under one agreement. The term "wet leasing" comes from aviation, where an aircraft is leased with its crew. Here, the machine comes with its crew too.

You define the output you need. We keep the machine productive and are responsible for its availability. The commercial model – a periodic fee, a rate per wheelset or a combination – is agreed for each project.

What is included

One agreement, complete capability

The machine

A suitable HYT wheel lathe, installed and commissioned at your site.

The crew

Trained HYT operators working to your shift pattern.

Maintenance

Preventive maintenance, breakdown attendance and spares management by HYT.

Reporting

Records of wheelsets machined and measurement results for your fleet systems.

When Machine as a Service makes sense

  • A new depot needs re-profiling capacity before a machine can be procured
  • Capital budgets are constrained but operating budgets are available
  • Demand is temporary – a fleet introduction, a backlog or a major overhaul campaign
  • The organisation prefers to outsource specialist maintenance activities
  • A trial period is needed before deciding on a permanent machine
  • In-house staff are not yet trained on CNC wheel lathes

Process

From enquiry to output
