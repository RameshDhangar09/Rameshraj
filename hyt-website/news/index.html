NCRTC issues Letter of Acceptance for fully automatic CNC underfloor wheel lathes

National Capital Region Transport Corporation has issued HYT a Letter of Acceptance for fully automatic CNC underfloor wheel lathes. The machines will re-profile wheelsets in the vehicle, so trains spend less time out of service.

Read more

OrdersOrder

Delhi Metro awards HYT a new CNC tandem underfloor wheel lathe

Delhi Metro Rail Corporation has awarded HYT a contract for a new CNC tandem underfloor wheel lathe. A tandem machine re-profiles two wheelsets of a bogie in one setting – an advantage for high-frequency metro fleets.

Read more

Indian Railways contract for nine CNC underfloor wheel lathes

Indian Railways has contracted HYT to supply nine CNC underfloor wheel lathes, adding in-vehicle re-profiling capacity to its maintenance network.
