About the machine

Built for the wheel shop

When wheelsets are removed for overhaul, wheel replacement or heavy repair, they reach the wheel shop in a steady stream – often with deep flats, shelling or hollow wear that call for substantial material removal. The YPL-02 is designed for that work.

Its portal structure carries the tool slides over the wheelset and takes the cutting forces into a closed, rigid frame. With a 2 × 55 kW main drive and a chip cross-section of 32–50 mm², the machine takes heavy cuts on hard, worn treads while holding the profile accurately.

The wheelset is rolled in on rails, brought to the machining position and clamped; both wheels are then measured and turned. For a related portal design, see the CNC Roller Type Portal Wheel Lathe YPL-04.

Key advantages

Why wheel shops choose a portal lathe

Heavy-cut capability

2 × 55 kW main drive and 32–50 mm² chip cross-section for efficient removal of deep defects.

Rigid portal frame

A closed structure for stable cutting and repeatable profiles on hard treads.

Broad wheelset envelope

Tread diameters 750–1250 mm, wheel widths 75–145 mm and axle lengths 1850–2750 mm, depending on gauge.

Material-saving profiles

Measurement before machining lets the CNC take only the depth needed, preserving wheel life.

Fits a wheel-shop flow

Works alongside wheelset tracks, measuring stations and presses in a continuous process.

No pit under a running line

The wheelset comes to the machine, so no track pit is needed in the wheel shop.

Operating principle

From worn wheelset to restored profile

  1. 01

    Roll in

    The removed wheelset is rolled into the machine along the shop track.

  2. 02

    Position

    It is raised to the machining position and aligned with the machine axis.

  3. 03

    Clamp & drive

    The wheelset is clamped securely and rotated by the main drive.

  4. 04

    Measure

    Both wheels are measured and the CNC calculates the cut required.

  5. 05

    Rough & finish

    Heavy roughing passes remove defects; a finishing pass restores the exact profile.

  6. 06

    Inspect & roll out

    The result is checked and recorded, and the wheelset leaves for assembly or storage.

Service and support

Engineered into your wheel shop

A portal lathe performs best when wheelsets reach it without waiting. HYT plans the machine's position in the shop together with wheelset tracks, storage and the next process steps, provides the foundation drawing and commissions the lathe on your wheelsets.

For larger facilities we deliver the complete process as a turnkey project or as part of wheel shop automation. Spare supply and maintenance contracts follow for the life of the machine.
