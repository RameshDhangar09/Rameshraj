Maintenance Services

Operation Contract: trained operators for your machine

HYT operators run the machine; maintenance remains with your own team or under a separate contract. You gain skilled operation without handing over machine upkeep.

Contenu en attente de validation par HYT: This service overlaps with Operation & Maintenance (O&M). HYT to confirm that both pages should remain and that the scope below (operation only, maintenance excluded) is correct.

How it works

Operation only – a clear, narrow scope

Some depots have a strong maintenance department but no operators trained on a CNC wheel lathe. Others face a temporary gap: a new machine arriving before staff are recruited, or a period of high re-profiling demand. An Operation Contract fills that gap with HYT operators who know the machine, its measuring system and its profile programs.

Our operators work to your shift pattern and maintenance plan. They position wheelsets, measure, select the correct profile, machine and record the result. They carry out operator-level care – cleaning, routine lubrication and daily checks – and report any abnormality to your maintenance team straight away.

Preventive maintenance, breakdown repair and spare parts are not part of this contract. They remain with your own team or can be placed with HYT under an AMC or CMC. If you prefer one contract for everything, choose Operation & Maintenance (O&M).

Included

  • Trained HYT operators for the agreed shifts
  • Wheelset measurement, profile selection and machining
  • Operator-level care: cleaning, routine lubrication and daily checks
  • Records of wheelsets machined and measurement results
  • Immediate reporting of faults to your maintenance team
  • Work to your depot's safety rules and permit-to-work system

Not included

  • Preventive maintenance of the machine
  • Breakdown diagnosis and repair
  • Spare parts
  • Civil, utility and track work
  • Shunting and movement of rolling stock, unless agreed
  • Retro-fitment and upgrades
