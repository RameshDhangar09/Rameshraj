About the machine

Portal machining with a roller drive

Portal wheel lathes machine wheelsets that have been removed from the vehicle. In the roller-type design, the wheelset is supported and rotated on rollers – the drive principle familiar from underfloor lathes – while the tool slides work from the portal structure.

That makes the YPL-04 an option for workshops that want the access of a portal machine for removed wheelsets combined with a roller drive. Whether the YPL-04 or the YPL-02 suits you better depends on your wheelsets, defect types and workflow; we advise on that during the enquiry.

If you would rather keep wheelsets in the vehicle, an underfloor wheel lathe is the alternative.

Key advantages

What the roller-type design offers

Roller-type drive

The wheelset is supported and rotated on rollers, a principle familiar to teams that run underfloor lathes.

Open access

The removed wheelset is easy to see, measure and inspect throughout the cycle.

Measured machining

Wheels are measured before cutting so that only the necessary material is removed.

Mixed fleets

Profile library and adaptable set-up for different wheelset types.

Wheel-shop integration

Can be placed in a line with measuring stations, presses and wheelset tracks.

Supported by the manufacturer

Spare parts, service contracts and upgrades directly from HYT.

Operating principle

How the YPL-04 works

  1. 01

    Roll in

    The removed wheelset is rolled into the machine on the shop track.

  2. 02

    Set on rollers

    It is lifted on to the drive rollers, which support and rotate it.

  3. 03

    Secure

    The wheelset is located and held steady for machining.

  4. 04

    Measure

    Both wheels are measured and the CNC determines the cut.

  5. 05

    Machine

    Tread and flange are turned to the selected profile.

  6. 06

    Verify & roll out

    Final dimensions are recorded and the wheelset leaves the machine.

Service and support

Choosing between portal designs

Before you decide, we compare the YPL-04 with the YPL-02 and with our surface wheel lathes using your actual wheelset data, defect types and daily volumes. The recommendation comes with a clear explanation of the trade-offs.

Installation, commissioning and operator training are carried out by HYT engineers, followed by the service contract that suits your workshop.
