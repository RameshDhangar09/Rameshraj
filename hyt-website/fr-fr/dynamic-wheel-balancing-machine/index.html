Dynamic Wheel Balancing Machine for railway wheels and wheelsets

An unbalanced wheel vibrates at every revolution, loading bearings and track and reducing ride comfort. The balancing machine measures how much unbalance there is and where, so it can be corrected before service.

About the machine

Why balance matters as speed rises

Unbalance is mass that is not evenly distributed around the axis of rotation. At low speed its effect is small. The resulting force, however, grows with the square of speed, and an unbalanced wheel becomes a source of vibration for bearings, bogie, track and car body.

A dynamic balancing machine spins the wheel or wheelset and measures the forces this creates. It determines both the amount and the angular position of the unbalance, in one or two planes, so the workshop knows exactly how much material to remove or add, and where.

Operators of passenger trains, metros and multiple units commonly specify balance limits for their wheels, because ride comfort, noise and component life depend on them.

Operating principle

From measurement to correction

  1. 01

    Load

    The wheel or wheelset is placed in the machine.

  2. 02

    Secure

    It is clamped or supported for rotation.

  3. 03

    Spin up

    The workpiece is brought to measuring speed.

  4. 04

    Measure

    Sensors record the forces caused by unbalance.

  5. 05

    Display

    The machine shows the amount and angular position of the correction in each plane.

  6. 06

    Correct & verify

    Material is removed or added, and a check run confirms the result.

Frequently asked questions
