Services

Services that keep railway machine tools productive for life

A wheel lathe earns its value over decades, not on the day it is handed over. HYT installs, maintains, repairs, upgrades and can even operate its machines, so your workshop keeps re-profiling wheels whenever the fleet needs it.

Why service matters

When the wheel lathe stops, trains wait

A wheel lathe sits on the critical path of fleet availability. If it is out of action, wheelsets with flats or thin flanges cannot be restored, vehicles are held back from traffic and the workshop falls behind its maintenance plan. The cost of a lost day is measured in trains, not in machine hours.

We design and build our machines in-house, so our service engineers know them from the foundation bolts to the CNC program. That knowledge shortens diagnosis, keeps repairs right first time and lets us recommend the spares and upgrades that genuinely extend machine life.

Every service below can be taken on its own or combined into a long-term contract. Tell us how your workshop runs and we will propose the level of support that fits – no more, no less.

  • Support from the engineers who designed and built the machine
  • Clear contract scopes, so you always know what is covered
  • Genuine spare parts identified against your machine's build records

Machine life cycle

How we support you across the machine life cycle

The right support changes as a machine moves from project to production to mid-life. We plan for each stage from the start.

  1. 01

    Before delivery

    Foundation and pit drawings, utility requirements and a training plan agreed early, so the site is ready when the machine arrives.

  2. 02

    Installation & commissioning

    Erection, alignment and trials on your own wheelsets, followed by a documented handover. Learn more.

  3. 03

    Early production

    Operator coaching, a recommended first-year spares list and quick answers while your team gains confidence.

  4. 04

    Planned maintenance

    Preventive visits and breakdown cover under an AMC, CMC or O&M contract.

  5. 05

    Mid-life renewal

    Retro-fitment of CNC, drives and measuring systems, major repairs or relocation to a new depot.

  6. 06

    Continuous capability

    Refresher training for new staff and updated profile programs as your fleet changes.
