Turnkey Solutions

Wheel and axle shops for rolling stock manufacturing facilities

Coach, locomotive and metro-car factories depend on a steady supply of finished wheelsets. HYT plans and equips the wheel and axle shop within the factory, matched to its production rhythm.

The facility

The wheelset supply inside a vehicle factory

A rolling stock factory builds car bodies, bogies and complete vehicles to a fixed delivery schedule. Wheelsets are an input that bogie assembly cannot do without, and they are safety-critical: every one must be machined, mounted and documented to the vehicle specification and the purchaser's approval requirements.

Factories that set up their own wheel and axle shop face a particular set of problems. Output must keep pace with bogie assembly rather than simply run at full capacity. Several vehicle projects may run at once, each with its own wheel and axle drawings. And the quality records for every wheelset must be ready when the vehicle is presented for acceptance.

HYT's role is clearly defined. We do not build car bodies or bogie frames. We plan, equip and commission the wheel and axle shop – machining, pressing, measuring and balancing – and connect it to the factory's material flow and quality system as one turnkey package.

What HYT delivers

Our part of the factory

Wheel and axle production equipment

Machines for axle turning, wheel boring and profiling, wheel pressing, measuring and dynamic balancing.

Integration with the plant

Shop layout, buffers and handover points aligned with bogie assembly and the factory's internal logistics.

Quality records

Measurement, pressing and balancing data organised per wheelset to support vehicle acceptance.

Process flow

From incoming parts to bogie assembly

  1. 01

    Incoming wheels and axles

    Forged or pre-machined wheels and axles are received, identified and allocated to the vehicle project they belong to.

  2. 02

    Axle machining

    Axles are turned to drawing on the CNC Axle Turning Lathe ATL-01.

  3. 03

    Wheel machining

    Hub bores, rim faces and profiles are machined on the CNC vertical turning and boring machine, with bores sized to the measured axle seats.

  4. 04

    Mounting

    Wheels are pressed on to the axle on a wheel press, and the force curve of each press-fit is recorded.

  5. 05

    Measurement and balancing

    Back-to-back distance and diameters are checked, and wheelsets are balanced where the vehicle specification requires it.

  6. 06

    Handover to bogie assembly

    Finished wheelsets go to the bogie line with their records, at the rate the assembly programme calls for.

Project delivery

How we deliver it

Our Turnkey (EPC) Projects approach, adapted to a factory that is often being built or extended at the same time.

  1. 01

    Production study

    Wheelset demand per vehicle project, product variants and the bogie assembly rhythm define capacity and layout.

  2. 02

    Building interface

    Foundation, crane and utility requirements are fed into the factory design while it is still on paper.

  3. 03

    Supply and installation

    Machines are built and tested in Pune, then installed and connected on site. See Installation & Commissioning.

  4. 04

    Ramp-up

    First-article wheelsets are produced and checked with your quality team before series output begins.

  5. 05

    Skills and support

    Operators and maintainers are trained through our Training programme, with spare parts and service contracts to follow.

Outcomes

Why factories use one partner for this shop

When the wheel and axle shop is engineered as one system, it delivers finished wheelsets at the rate the bogie line needs – and the records that vehicle acceptance requires arrive with them.

  • Wheelset output matched to the assembly programme
  • Consistent interference fits and geometry across vehicle projects
  • Complete per-wheelset documentation for acceptance
  • Changeovers between product variants planned into machines and fixtures
  • One contract for the shop's equipment, integration and support
