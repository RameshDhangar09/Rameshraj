What our customers have in common

Operators who cannot afford idle trains

Our customers run high-utilization fleets on tight maintenance schedules. For them, a wheel lathe is not an occasional tool but a daily bottleneck: every hour a train waits for re-profiling is an hour it is not earning revenue or carrying passengers.

That is why they look for more than a machine. They need foundations designed correctly the first time, operators trained on their own vehicles and a service partner who responds when it matters. See the details in our executed projects and projects under execution.
