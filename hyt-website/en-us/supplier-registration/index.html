Work With Us

Supplier registration

Every HYT machine is only as reliable as its parts. We welcome capable suppliers who share our standards for quality, delivery and responsible business.

What we buy

Supply categories

We source a wide range of materials, components and services for our wheel lathes, wheel and axle shop machines, depot equipment and turnkey projects.

  • Castings and forgings
  • Machined components
  • Fabricated and welded structures
  • Electrical and control panels
  • CNC systems, drives and motors
  • Hydraulic and pneumatic systems
  • Bearings and transmission parts
  • Measuring and sensor equipment
  • Civil, erection and installation services
  • Logistics, packing and transport

Our expectations

What we look for in a supplier

Quality

Parts that meet drawing and specification every time, with inspection records and traceability where required.

Delivery

Realistic commitments and early warning if a date is at risk. Our project schedules depend on yours.

Documentation

Test certificates, material certificates and manuals supplied with the goods, complete and correct.

Safety and environment

Safe working practices and responsible environmental management, in line with our ISO 45001 and ISO 14001 systems.

Compliance

Ethical, lawful business in line with our Code of Conduct and statutory requirements.

Communication

A named contact, quick responses and open discussion when problems arise.

Process

How registration works

  1. 01

    Register

    Complete the form below with your company details, supply categories and certifications.

  2. 02

    Review

    Our purchasing team reviews your profile against our current and planned requirements.

  3. 03

    Assessment

    Where there is a match, we may request documents, samples or a visit to your facility.

  4. 04

    Approval

    Approved suppliers are added to our vendor list for the relevant categories.

  5. 05

    Inquiries and orders

    You receive inquiries when a requirement matches your capabilities. Performance is reviewed regularly.

Register your company

Registration does not guarantee an inquiry or an order, but it ensures our purchasing team knows your capabilities when a requirement arises.
