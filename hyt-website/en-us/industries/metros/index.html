Industries

Solutions for Metros and Rapid Transit

Metro trains run all day and are maintained at night. HYT wheel lathes and depot equipment fit into those short windows, so the full fleet is ready for the morning peak.

The challenge

High frequency, short windows, no spare trains

Metro and rapid transit fleets are sized tightly to the timetable. Frequent acceleration and braking, tight curves and high daily mileage wear wheels quickly, and ride comfort and noise are noticed immediately by passengers and neighbours.

Maintenance usually happens overnight in a depot where space is limited and every pit is planned years in advance. A wheel lathe must re-profile a whole train within that window, produce repeatable results across the fleet and be ready again the next night. When it is out of service, trains queue up and availability targets slip.

How HYT helps

Wheel maintenance that fits the night shift

Tandem machines

Two synchronized underfloor lathes machine both wheelsets of a bogie in one setting, cutting the time per train.

Automatic cycles

Measure, calculate and cut with minimal operator input for consistent results across the fleet.

Fleet data

Machining reports for every wheelset support condition-based maintenance and audits.

Ride quality

Accurate profiles and matched diameters help keep noise and vibration down.

Complete depots

Wheel lathes, train washing, shunting and armature equipment from one partner.

Service you can plan on

Maintenance and operation contracts with defined response times and spares on hand.
