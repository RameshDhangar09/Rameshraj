About the machine

Two wheelsets, one setting

On a single underfloor lathe, the wheelsets of a bogie are machined one after the other: the vehicle is moved, the next axle is positioned and the cycle starts again. The YUL-12T removes that step. Two machines installed in one pit work in synchronized tandem, so both wheelsets of a bogie are re-profiled together.

One unit is fixed; the second traverses along the pit to suit the bogie wheelbase. A single installation can therefore serve vehicles with different bogie designs – from locomotives to coaches and multiple units – without changes to the foundation.

HYT figures show that tandem operation reduces re-profiling time by 50% for coaches and 33% for locomotives compared with a single machine. For a platform positioned for higher automation, compare the YUGLX.

Key advantages

What tandem operation changes in the depot

Shorter re-profiling time

50% less time for coaches and 33% less for locomotives than with a single machine (HYT figures).

Adjustable wheelbase

The traversing unit is positioned to the bogie, so one pit serves several vehicle types.

Fewer vehicle moves

No shunting between the two axles of a bogie – less time, less handling and less risk.

Wide diameter range

Tread diameters from 540 to 1500 mm cover a broad mix of vehicles.

Flexible cutting speed

20–120 m/min to suit wheel material and wear condition.

Matched wheels across the bogie

Both wheelsets are measured and machined in the same setting, which makes diameter matching across the bogie easier to control.

Operating principle

One bogie, one cycle

  1. 01

    Position

    The vehicle is drawn over the pit until the bogie stands above the fixed machine.

  2. 02

    Set the wheelbase

    The traversing machine moves along the pit to line up with the second wheelset.

  3. 03

    Lift & clamp

    Drive rollers take the load of both wheelsets and the axleboxes are clamped.

  4. 04

    Measure

    All four wheels are measured and the CNC determines the cut for each.

  5. 05

    Machine

    Both wheelsets are re-profiled in synchronized operation.

  6. 06

    Verify & move on

    Final measurements are recorded, the wheelsets are lowered and the vehicle moves to the next bogie.

Customer applications

Where a tandem lathe pays off

Tandem machines earn their place wherever many vehicles must be re-profiled in short windows – one reason metros invest in them. Delhi Metro Rail Corporation, for example, has awarded HYT a contract for a new CNC tandem underfloor wheel lathe.

Metro depots

Long, high-utilization fleets where every hour of availability counts.

EMU and suburban car sheds

Many multiple-unit bogies re-profiled in short maintenance windows.

Locomotive sheds

Locomotive bogies handled with fewer moves over the pit.

Coaching depots

Faster turnaround for long passenger rakes between trips.

Service and support

Two machines, one point of responsibility

A tandem installation needs a longer pit, precise rail alignment and close coordination between the two units. HYT supplies pit and foundation drawings, coordinates the civil interface with your contractor and commissions both machines together on your own vehicles.

After handover, a Comprehensive Maintenance Contract (CMC), spare supply and operator training keep the pair running in step.

Frequently asked questions

HYT figures show re-profiling time reduced by 50% for coaches and 33% for locomotives compared with a single underfloor machine. Actual savings depend on your vehicles and depot routine.

One machine is fixed and the other traverses along the pit. It is positioned to match the wheelbase of the bogie over the pit, so vehicles with different bogies can use the same installation.

The YUL-12T covers tread diameters from 540 to 1500 mm. Wheel profiles and gauge are configured to your fleet.

Both are tandem underfloor lathes. The YUL-12T is the established model with the data shown on this page. The YUGLX is positioned as the next-generation tandem platform with a higher degree of automation. We will compare both for your fleet.

If your re-profiling volume is moderate, a single YUL-08 may be sufficient. Share fleet size and re-profiling intervals and we will compare both options.
