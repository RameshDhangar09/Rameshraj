Integrated Policy Statement

Our commitments on quality, environment, occupational health and safety and business integrity, consistent with our ISO 9001, ISO 14001 and ISO 45001 management systems.

Our purpose

HYT Engineering Company Pvt. Ltd. designs, manufactures, installs and services CNC wheel lathes, wheel and axle shop machines, depot equipment and turnkey workshops for railways, metros and industry. The machines we build help keep rolling stock safe. That responsibility shapes the way we manage quality, the environment, and the health and safety of everyone involved in our work.

Quality

We commit to delivering machines and services that meet customer requirements and applicable standards, on time and with full documentation. To achieve this we will:

  • understand and review customer requirements before we commit to them;
  • control design, purchasing, manufacturing, installation and service through documented processes;
  • verify our machines through inspection and testing, including acceptance on customer wheelsets;
  • measure customer satisfaction and learn from feedback and non-conformities;
  • continually improve the effectiveness of our quality management system to ISO 9001:2015.

Environment

We commit to protecting the environment and preventing pollution across our operations and products. We will:

  • comply with applicable environmental laws, permits and other requirements;
  • use energy, water and materials efficiently;
  • minimize waste, and handle oils, coolants and hazardous materials safely;
  • consider environmental performance when designing machines and selecting suppliers;
  • continually improve our environmental management system to ISO 14001:2015.

Occupational health and safety

We commit to providing safe and healthy working conditions to prevent work-related injury and ill health – in our works and on customer sites. We will:

  • comply with applicable health and safety laws and customer site rules;
  • identify hazards, assess risks and eliminate or reduce them using the hierarchy of controls;
  • provide training, information and suitable protective equipment;
  • consult and involve employees and their representatives in safety matters;
  • design our machines with the safety of operators and maintenance staff in mind;
  • continually improve our occupational health and safety management system to ISO 45001:2018.

Business integrity and anti-bribery

We commit to conducting business honestly and fairly. HYT prohibits bribery and corruption in all forms, whether involving public officials or private parties, directly or through intermediaries. We comply with applicable anti-corruption and competition laws and respect the procurement rules of our customers. These commitments are explained in our Code of Conduct and Business Conduct Guidelines.

Putting this policy into practice

Management sets measurable objectives under this policy, provides the resources needed to achieve them and reviews performance regularly. Every employee is responsible for working in line with this policy, and we expect the same of our suppliers and subcontractors.

This policy is communicated to all employees, made available to interested parties on this website and reviewed periodically to make sure it remains suitable. Details of our certificates are on the Certifications page.
