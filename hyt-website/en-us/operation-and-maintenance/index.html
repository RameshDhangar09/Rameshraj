Maintenance Services

Operation & Maintenance (O&M) contracts for wheel lathes

HYT operators run the machine and HYT technicians maintain it. You receive re-profiled wheelsets on schedule without having to build a specialist lathe team of your own.

How it works

One team responsible for output and machine health

Running a CNC wheel lathe well takes more than pressing start. Operators must position vehicles safely, interpret measurement results, choose the right profile and recognize early signs of trouble. Maintaining the machine requires mechanical, hydraulic, electrical and CNC skills that many depots do not have in-house.

Under an O&M contract, HYT provides both. Our operators work to your shift pattern and maintenance plan; our technicians keep the machine in condition with planned maintenance and attend breakdowns. Because the same organization is responsible for operation and maintenance, there is no argument about whether a stoppage was caused by the operator or by the machine.

O&M suits new depots that have not yet trained a lathe team, operators that outsource specialist activities by policy, and high-utilization machines where every hour of availability counts.

Included

What an O&M contract includes

Machine operation

Trained HYT operators run the machine to the shift pattern and workload agreed with you.

Preventive maintenance

Daily, weekly and periodic maintenance tasks carried out to the HYT maintenance schedule.

Breakdown attendance

Faults are diagnosed and rectified by HYT technicians, with escalation to our engineering team.

Records and reports

Wheelsets machined, measurement results, stoppages and maintenance work recorded and reported.

Spares planning

Stock levels monitored and replenishment planned; supply of parts as defined in the contract.

Safe working

Work carried out to agreed safety procedures and your depot's permit-to-work rules.

What is not included (unless agreed in the contract)

Clear exclusions avoid surprises. The following are normally outside an O&M scope:

  • Civil and structural repairs to the pit, building or track
  • Power, compressed air and other utilities up to the machine connection point
  • Shunting and movement of rolling stock to and from the machine
  • Damage caused by accidents, third-party misuse, fire, flooding or supply faults
  • Spare parts and consumables beyond the quantities defined in the contract
  • Retro-fitment, upgrades or modifications to the machine

Contract cycle

How an O&M contract runs

  1. 01

    Scope definition

    Shifts, expected workload, reporting format and the split of responsibilities are agreed.

  2. 02

    Mobilisation

    The HYT team, tools and initial spares are deployed and the machine's condition is recorded.

  3. 03

    Daily operation

    Vehicles are re-profiled according to your maintenance plan, with results logged for every wheelset.

  4. 04

    Planned maintenance

    Maintenance tasks are scheduled into low-demand periods to protect availability.

  5. 05

    Review

    Output, stoppages and machine condition are reviewed with you at agreed intervals.

  6. 06

    Renewal or transition

    The contract is renewed, or your own staff are trained to take over operation.
