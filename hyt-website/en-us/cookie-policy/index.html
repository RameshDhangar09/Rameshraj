Cookie Policy

Short version: this website does not set cookies, does not use analytics and does not track you. This page explains the details.

Draft for legal review – October 2026

1. Summary

  • We do not set cookies on this website.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising, social media or other tracking tools.
  • Fonts and images are served from our own website, not from third-party services.
  • Only strictly necessary technical storage is used, as described below.
  • Because no optional cookies are used, we do not show a cookie consent banner.

2. What cookies and similar technologies are

Cookies are small text files that a website stores in your browser. Similar technologies include the browser's local and session storage, pixels and device fingerprinting. They can be used to make a website work, to remember your preferences, to measure visits or to track you across websites for advertising.

Data protection and electronic communications laws – including the EU ePrivacy rules and the UK Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations – require consent for any such technology that is not strictly necessary to provide the service you have asked for.

3. What this website uses

This website uses only what is strictly necessary to display pages and make forms work. Your browser may hold small amounts of information on your own device for these technical functions – for example while you complete a form. This information is not used to identify or track you and is not shared with us or anyone else.

When you load a page, our hosting provider's servers process technical data such as your IP address in order to deliver the page and protect the site. This is not a cookie; it is described in our Privacy Policy.

4. Forms and downloads

When you submit a form, the details you enter are sent to our form-processing provider and on to HYT. No cookie is set in the process. If we ever add a spam-protection service that relies on cookies, we will update this policy and ask for consent where required. Downloading a catalog does not set a cookie.

5. Links to other websites

Some links – for example to a map service or an external publication – take you to other websites. Those websites may set their own cookies under their own policies, which we do not control.

6. If this changes

If we decide to add analytics, embedded videos, embedded maps or any other feature that sets non-essential cookies or similar technologies, we will:

  • update this policy first, listing each cookie, its provider, purpose and duration;
  • show a consent banner so that non-essential cookies are set only after you agree;
  • make it as easy to refuse or withdraw consent as to give it.

7. Managing cookies in your browser

You can view, block and delete cookies and stored site data in your browser settings at any time. Blocking strictly necessary storage may stop some features, such as forms, from working correctly.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to info@hytengineering.com with "Data protection" in the subject line.
