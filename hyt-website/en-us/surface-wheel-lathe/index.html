Product overview

What is the CNC Surface Wheel Lathe?

The CNC Surface Wheel Lathe is a high-precision machine designed for the reprofiling of railway wheelsets that have been disassembled from the rolling stock and are machined off the vehicle.

As railway wheels run on track over time, their tread profiles wear unevenly, causing vibration, noise, reduced braking efficiency and safety risks. The CNC Surface Wheel Lathe restores each wheel's profile back to the exact OEM specification in a single, automated cycle.

Wheelsets from all types of rolling stock – locomotives, coaches, wagons, metro trains and EMUs – can be machined on this lathe. A fully integrated pre- and post-machining measurement system ensures that every wheel is machined to exact tolerances, minimizing material removal and extending wheel life.

Model range

YSL-08

The YSL-08 is the highest-output model of the range. Two tool posts reprofile both wheels simultaneously, driven by a main drive of 2 × 45 or 55 kW with a chip cross-section of up to 20 mm². A wheelset is completed in 14–16 minutes floor to floor, holding the difference in diameter of both wheels below 0.15 mm.

Model range

YSL-12

The YSL-12 also reprofiles both wheels simultaneously, with a 2 × 37 kW main drive and a chip cross-section of up to 16 mm². It covers the same tread diameters (750–1250 mm) and wheel widths (75–145 mm) as the YSL-08, with a floor-to-floor time of 16–18 minutes per wheelset.

Model range

YSL-21

The YSL-21 works with a single tool post and a 1 × 22 kW main drive, with a chip cross-section of up to 10 mm². It takes tread diameters of 750–1250 mm and wheel widths of 75–145 mm, with a floor-to-floor time of 50–60 minutes per wheelset.

Operating principle

How a removed wheelset is re-profiled

  1. 01

    Roll in

    The removed wheelset is rolled along the workshop track into the machine.

  2. 02

    Lift & clamp

    It is raised to the machining position and clamped securely.

  3. 03

    Measure

    Diameter, flange height and flange thickness are recorded on both wheels.

  4. 04

    Program

    The operator selects the target profile and the CNC calculates the cut required.

  5. 05

    Turn

    Tread and flange are machined, followed by brake discs where the configuration includes them.

  6. 06

    Check & roll out

    The wheelset is re-measured, the result documented and the wheelset rolled out for assembly.

Main features

  • Floor-level machine on a level foundation – no track pit
  • Wheelset loading on rails for safe, controlled handling
  • Tool slides for the left and right wheels
  • Brake-disc machining on suitable configurations
  • Profile library stored in the CNC for mixed fleets
  • Choice of main drive power on the YSL-08
  • Chip removal from the cutting zone
  • Guarded working area with a clear operator view

Customer applications

Where a surface lathe makes sense

Locomotive workshops

Re-profiling wheelsets during periodic overhaul, when bogies are already stripped.

Coach and EMU overhaul

Wheel and brake-disc machining in one setting on removed wheelsets.

Wagon repair shops

Removing flats and hollow wear from wagon wheelsets between overhauls.

Workshops without a pit

Buildings where civil works beneath the track are impractical or too costly.

Service and support

From layout to lifetime service

Because the machine stands at floor level, installation is simpler to plan than for a pit lathe. HYT supplies the foundation drawing, checks the workshop layout for wheelset access and craneage, and commissions the lathe on your own wheelsets.

Operators and maintainers are trained on site. Afterwards, spare supply, an Annual Maintenance Contract and retro-fitment keep the machine productive for many years.
