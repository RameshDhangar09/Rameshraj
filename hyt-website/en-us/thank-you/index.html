What happens next

Three steps from here

  1. 01

    Acknowledgement

    Your submission is logged and passed to the responsible team – sales, service, purchasing or HR.

  2. 02

    Review by a specialist

    An HYT specialist reviews what you sent. If we need more information, such as wheel profiles or drawings, we will ask.

  3. 03

    Response

    We reply by email or telephone. For product inquiries this may be a recommendation, a meeting request or a technical proposal.
