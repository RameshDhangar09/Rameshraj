Wheel & Axle Shop Machines

Wheel Press for mounting and dismounting railway wheelsets

Wheels are held on their axles by an interference fit, not by fasteners. A wheel press makes and breaks that fit under controlled force – and the quality of the joint depends on it.

About the machine

Making and breaking the interference fit

A railway wheel is pressed on to its axle seat with a specified interference: the bore is slightly smaller than the seat. During mounting, the force rises as the wheel travels along the seat, and that force profile shows whether the joint is sound.

HYT wheel presses are built for both directions of this work. In mounting, the press positions the wheel and drives it on to the seat to the specified back-to-back distance. In dismounting, it applies the force needed to remove worn wheels from axles that will be reused.

Pressing brings together the work of other wheel-shop machines: bores from the vertical turning and boring machine, seats from the axle turning lathe and final checks on the wheel measuring station.

Key advantages

What a good press-fit needs

Controlled force

Press force applied smoothly and kept within the set limits.

Press-fit records

The force–displacement curve can be recorded for each wheel as evidence of a sound joint.

Correct wheel position

Wheels pressed to the specified back-to-back distance.

Mounting and dismounting

Configured for mounting, dismounting or both, according to your process.

High forces, handled safely

Guarding and interlocks around the pressing zone.

More than wheels

Tooling can be adapted for other parts pressed on to axles, such as gears or brake discs.

Process

Mounting a wheel, step by step

  1. 01

    Prepare

    Bore and seat dimensions are checked and the interference confirmed.

  2. 02

    Load the axle

    The axle is placed and supported in the press.

  3. 03

    Position the wheel

    The wheel is aligned with the wheel seat.

  4. 04

    Press

    The wheel is driven on to the seat while force and travel are monitored.

  5. 05

    Check position

    The back-to-back distance is verified.

  6. 06

    Document

    The press-fit result is recorded for the wheelset file.

  • Hydraulic press frame sized to the required force

  • Guarded pressing zone with interlocks
  • Interfaces for wheelset handling equipment

Where wheel presses are needed

Mounting new wheels and removing worn ones during overhaul.

Rolling stock manufacturers

Overhaul workshops

Re-wheeling axles that remain serviceable.

Metro depots

In-house wheelset assembly for metro fleets.

Service and support

A press you can rely on

HYT sizes the press to your wheels, axles and interference specification, installs and commissions it, and trains your team on pressing and documentation.

Hydraulic components and seals are available through spare supply, and the press can be integrated into wheel shop automation for a continuous wheelset flow.
