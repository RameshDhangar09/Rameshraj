Maintenance Services

Spare parts and technical support

Genuine parts matched to your machine's build records, and engineers on the phone who can help your team fit them correctly. Fewer delays, fewer wrong parts, longer machine life.

Why genuine parts

The right part, first time

Machines built over several decades and configured for different gauges, profiles and vehicle types are rarely identical. The roller, seal or sensor that fits one machine may not fit the next. We identify spares against your machine's serial number and build records, so the part that arrives is the part that fits.

Genuine parts are made to the original drawings and materials. That protects machining accuracy, avoids damage to mating components and keeps your warranty and contract terms intact.

For workshops that maintain their machines themselves, our engineers are available by phone and email to advise on fault-finding, part replacement and settings.

What we supply

Spares and support for the whole machine

Mechanical parts

Rollers, bearings, slides, ball screws, clamping elements and other wear parts.

Hydraulic and pneumatic

Valves, cylinders, pumps, seals and fittings for the machine's fluid systems.

Electrical and CNC

Drives, motors, sensors, control components and cabinet parts.

Measuring system parts

Probes and components for the wheel profile measuring system.

Recommended spares lists

Lists of critical and wear parts to hold on site, ranked by risk and lead time.

Obsolescence planning

Early notice when components are being discontinued, with alternatives or upgrade paths.

Ordering

From inquiry to dispatch

  1. 01

    Identify

    Send the machine serial number and the part number from the manual, or a photo of the part.

  2. 02

    Confirm

    We check the part against your machine's records and confirm the correct item.

  3. 03

    Quote

    You receive a quotation with price and delivery time.

  4. 04

    Dispatch

    On receipt of your order, the part is packed and dispatched from our Pune works.

  5. 05

    Support

    Our engineers advise on fitting by phone, or attend on site if you prefer.

Plan ahead

Hold the parts that stop the machine

Some parts wear predictably; others fail rarely but stop the machine when they do. Holding a small stock of the critical items on site can turn a week of downtime into a few hours. Our recommended spares list ranks parts by how critical they are to operation and how long they take to source, so you can decide what to keep in your store and what to order when needed.

We review the list with you as the machine ages, when components are discontinued, and after a retro-fitment changes the machine's configuration.

Information to include with a spares inquiry

  • Machine model and serial number
  • Part number and description from the manual
  • Photographs of the part and its nameplate, if any
  • Quantity required
  • Delivery address and any import requirements
  • Urgency – is the machine stopped?
