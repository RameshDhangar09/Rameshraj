Who we are

Precision that keeps fleets moving

A railway wheel carries tonnes on a contact patch the size of a coin. Its profile decides ride quality, rail wear, energy consumption and – above all – safety. HYT Engineering exists to restore that profile quickly and accurately, and to give workshops the machines they need to build and overhaul wheelsets with confidence.

Headquartered in Pune, India, we have spent more than four decades concentrating on one discipline: railway wheel and axle machining. Our engineers design every machine in-house, build it in our own facility, commission it on site and support it for decades after handover.

  • Specialist focus on wheelset machining and workshop equipment
  • Machines engineered around Indian and international wheel profiles and gauges
  • Single-point responsibility from foundation drawing to lifetime service

Flagship technology

CNC underfloor wheel lathes

An underfloor wheel lathe re-profiles wheels while the wheelset stays in the vehicle. The train is drawn over a pit, the axle is lifted on drive rollers and both wheels are machined at the same time – no bogie drop, no brake gear removal, no long shutdown.

HYT underfloor lathes combine rigid machine structures with automatic profile measurement, so the CNC removes only the material that is needed. The result is more re-profiling cycles per wheel, longer wheel life and more trains available for service.

  • Single machines (YUL-08, YUGL) and synchronized tandem pairs (YUL-12T, YUGLX)
  • Both wheels of a wheelset machined simultaneously
  • Suitable for locomotives, coaches, EMUs, metro cars and wagons

Wheel & axle shop

Vertical turning lathes and axle machining

New wheelsets and major overhauls begin in the wheel and axle shop. Our CNC vertical turning and boring machines finish wheel discs, tires and bores to tight tolerances, while dedicated axle lathes turn and burnish journals for reliable bearing seats.

Combined with wheel presses, measuring stations and dynamic balancing machines, these machines form a complete, traceable process from raw component to certified wheelset.

Technology & engineering

Engineering you can measure

Every HYT machine is designed around the realities of a railway workshop: heavy, worn wheels, demanding turnaround times and operators who need clear, safe controls.

Automatic profile measurement

Wheel diameter, flange height and thickness measured before cutting, so only necessary material is removed.

Proven CNC platforms

Industrial CNC systems with railway-specific cycles and on-screen profile libraries.

Rigid machine structures

Heavy fabricated and stress-relieved structures for stable cutting on hard, worn treads.

Safety by design

Interlocked guarding, clear operator zones and compliance with ISO 45001 practice.
