Responsibility

Corporate Social Responsibility

Our machines are built by people from the community around our works in Pune. We want that community to grow stronger alongside the company.

Our approach

Responsibility that starts close to home

Engineering companies depend on skilled people, a healthy environment and stable communities. Our CSR approach focuses on areas where an industrial manufacturer can make a lasting, practical difference rather than one-off gestures.

We aim to choose projects carefully, work with credible partners, involve our employees and report honestly on what has been achieved.

Focus areas

Where we want to make a difference

Education

Supporting schools and students, with an emphasis on science, mathematics and engineering.

Skill development

Helping young people gain the technical and vocational skills that manufacturing needs.

Environment

Reducing the footprint of our own operations under ISO 14001 and supporting local environmental initiatives.

Health & wellbeing

Promoting health and safety for employees, their families and the wider community.

Our people

Employees who take part

Responsibility is also part of everyday life at our works. Employee events such as blood donation camps, Safety Week and International Yoga Day bring colleagues together around health, safety and service to others.

Photographs of these events can be found on the Life at HYT page.
