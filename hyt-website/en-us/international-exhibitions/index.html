Media & Resources

HYT at international rail exhibitions

Rail exhibitions put us face to face with the operators, project teams and engineers who maintain fleets around the world. We go to listen as much as to present.

Why we exhibit

Conversations that shape better workshops

A wheel lathe or a wheel shop is a long-term decision. The operators who make it want to understand the engineering behind the machine, the people who will support it and how it will fit their own fleet, gauge and depot layout. That conversation starts best in person.

International exhibitions let us meet railways, metro operators, rolling stock manufacturers and consultants from many regions in a few days. We learn how maintenance practices differ between networks, which problems keep workshop managers awake and where new lines and depots are being planned. That knowledge feeds straight back into our machine design and our turnkey projects.
