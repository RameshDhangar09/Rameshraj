Certifications

Our quality, environmental and occupational health and safety management systems are independently certified. Here is what each certificate means for customers and partners.

Certified management systems

Three standards, one way of working

Each standard is audited by an independent certification body. Together they cover how we build machines, how we treat the environment and how we protect people.

ISO 9001:2015 – Quality management

Requires a documented, audited process for understanding customer requirements, controlling design, purchasing and production, and correcting problems. For you, it means consistent machines and traceable records.

ISO 14001:2015 – Environmental management

Requires us to identify the environmental impact of our operations – energy, waste, emissions, coolants and oils – and to control and reduce it. For you, it means a supplier that manages its footprint.

ISO 45001:2018 – Occupational health & safety

Requires us to identify hazards and prevent injury and ill health in our works and on customer sites. For you, it means safer installation and service work in your depot.

For buyers and auditors

Using our certificates in your supplier approval

Railway and metro tenders often require proof of certified management systems. Copies of our current certificates, including scope, certification body and validity dates, will be available for download on this page.

If you need certificates for a tender or a supplier audit sooner, contact us and we will send them directly. To see how ISO 9001 works in practice on the shop floor, read about our quality process, and for our commitments in each area see the Policy Statement.
