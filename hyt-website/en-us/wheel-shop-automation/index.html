Turnkey Projects

Wheel shop automation

Automation connects the machines of a wheel shop into one process: wheelsets move safely between workstations, measurements flow from machine to machine, and every component carries a complete history.

Why automate

From separate machines to one connected process

In many wheel shops, each machine works well on its own, but the process between them does not. Heavy wheelsets are moved by crane and hand, measurements are written down and re-entered, and matching a wheel bore to an axle seat depends on careful paperwork. Every manual step costs time and adds a chance of error on a safety-critical component.

Wheel shop automation is a solution, not a single machine. It links measuring, machining, pressing and balancing so that wheelsets move safely between workstations, data passes directly from one machine to the next, and every wheel and axle can be traced from receipt to release.

The approach suits railway and metro wheel shops and rolling stock manufacturers – anywhere throughput, consistency and traceability matter.

Building blocks

Four elements of an automated wheel shop

Automated handling

Wheelsets and components transferred between workstations with less crane work and manual lifting.

Integrated measurement

Wheels, axles and wheelsets measured at defined points, with results stored automatically.

Data flow between machines

Measured values passed directly to the next machine – for example, bore and seat data used for pressing.

Traceability

Each component identified and its measurements, machining and pressing records kept together.

Process flow

A wheelset's journey through an automated shop

  1. 01

    Receipt and identification

    Wheels, axles or wheelsets are identified and registered when they enter the shop.

  2. 02

    Measurement

    A wheel measuring station records the dimensions needed for the next operations.

  3. 03

    Machining

    Wheels are machined on a CNC vertical turning machine; axles on axle turning and journal burnishing lathes.

  4. 04

    Pressing

    A wheel press mounts wheels on the axle, with the press-fit record linked to the components.

  5. 05

    Balancing

    A dynamic wheel balancing machine checks and corrects imbalance.

  6. 06

    Final inspection and release

    The complete record travels with the wheelset into service.
