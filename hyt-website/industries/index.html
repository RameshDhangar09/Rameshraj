What links them

One discipline: precise machining of heavy rotating parts

A locomotive wheelset, a metro bogie, a new wheel disc on a production line and a large industrial ring have something in common. Each is heavy, each turns under load, and each must be machined accurately for the system around it to run safely and efficiently.

HYT has concentrated on that problem since moving into CNC wheel lathe technology. The same engineering know-how – rigid machine structures, accurate measurement, robust CNC control and careful foundation design – serves every industry on this page. What changes is the configuration, the throughput and the way the machine fits into your workshop.

Because we design and build every machine in-house, we can adapt it to unusual wheel sizes, heavy axle loads, restricted depot space or a particular production rhythm – and we remain responsible for it after handover.

Why it matters

What every customer needs from a machine partner

Availability

Short re-profiling and machining cycles keep vehicles and production lines moving.

Accuracy

Measurement before and after cutting removes only the material needed and documents the result.

Safety

Correct wheel profiles reduce derailment risk; guarded machines protect the people who run them.

Life-cycle support

Service, spares and upgrades for the decades a machine stays in use.
