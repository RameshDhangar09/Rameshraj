Solutions for Defence

Defence logistics depends on rolling stock and heavy equipment that must be ready when called upon. HYT machines help keep wheelsets and large rotating components serviceable.

Content pending HYT approval: No defence customers or defence-specific products are verified. This page describes capability only. Please confirm whether HYT wishes to feature this sector, which machines to show and whether any customers or projects may be named.

The challenge

Readiness, self-reliance and long service lives

Defence organisations move heavy equipment by rail and maintain their own wagons, coaches and locomotives in dedicated workshops. These fleets may run less often than commercial traffic, but they must be serviceable at short notice, and their wheelsets must meet the same safety limits as any other.

Military workshops also repair large rotating components such as rings, hubs and heavy wheels. They value equipment that is robust, simple to maintain, supported locally for decades and supplied with complete documentation. Confidentiality of drawings and site information is essential.

How HYT helps

Capability for defence workshops

Wheelset re-profiling

Underfloor and surface wheel lathes restore wheel profiles on wagons, coaches and locomotives used for military rail logistics.

Heavy vertical turning

CNC vertical turning and boring machines for large-diameter rotating components and wheels.

Depot mobility

Rail-cum-road shunters move wagons and coaches within sidings and workshops.

Designed and built in India

Engineering and manufacturing in Pune, with spares and service support from within India.

Confidential handling

Drawings, site data and project information handled according to contract requirements.

Full documentation

Manuals, drawings, inspection records and spare-parts lists supplied with every machine.

Relevant products

Machines suited to defence applications

CNC Surface Wheel Lathe

Floor-level CNC lathe for re-profiling removed wheelsets and brake discs, available as models YSL-08, YSL-12 and YSL-21.

Learn more

CNC Underfloor Wheel Lathe (YUL-08)

Single-axle pit lathe that re-profiles both wheels of a wheelset in the vehicle, without dropping bogies or removing brake gear.

Learn more

CNC Vertical Turning & Boring Machine

Vertical turning and boring machine for loose railway wheels and tyres – profile, rim faces and hub bore – in new production and overhaul.

Rail cum Road Vehicle (Shunter)

Dual-mode shunter that moves rolling stock on rails and drives on roads between tracks – an alternative to locomotive shunting in depots.
