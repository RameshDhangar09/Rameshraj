Industries

Solutions for Heavy Engineering

Large rotating components and industrial rail fleets need the same accuracy as a main-line railway. HYT applies its wheel machining expertise to heavy engineering plants.

Content pending HYT approval: No heavy engineering customers or sector-specific products are verified. This page describes capability only. Please confirm which machines and applications HYT wishes to feature and whether any customers or projects may be named.

The challenge

Big parts, heavy loads, costly downtime

Heavy engineering works machine large rotating parts – rings, flanges, hubs, gear blanks and wheels – where size and weight make every set-up slow and every error expensive. Machines must be rigid enough to cut hard material accurately and simple enough for the shop floor to run efficiently.

Many industrial sites, such as steel plants, ports, mines and power stations, also run their own railway fleets. Their wagons and locomotives carry heavy loads on short, demanding routes, and their wheels wear quickly. Sending wheelsets away for re-profiling can stop production logistics for days.

How HYT helps

Railway-grade machining for industry

Vertical turning and boring

CNC vertical turning lathes for large-diameter components, with rigid structures for heavy cuts.

On-site wheel re-profiling

Surface and portal wheel lathes let captive rail fleets re-profile wheels in their own workshop.

Wheel pressing

Presses for mounting and removing wheels, gears and similar press-fit components.

Balancing

Dynamic balancing for wheels and rotating assemblies to reduce vibration and wear.

Motor armature work

Armature machines for maintaining traction and large electric motors.

Service and spares

Maintenance contracts and genuine spares to protect production uptime.
