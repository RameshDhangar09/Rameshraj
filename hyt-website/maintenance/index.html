Services

Maintenance services and contracts

Six ways to keep your HYT machine available, from a call-based service agreement to full operation and maintenance by our own team. Compare the scopes and choose what fits your depot.

Planned maintenance

Planned maintenance costs less than downtime

A wheel lathe works hard: heavy wheelsets, hard and worn treads, abrasive chips and long shifts. Guideways, rollers, hydraulics and measuring systems all need regular attention. Left alone, small faults become breakdowns – usually at the moment the fleet needs the machine most.

Our maintenance contracts put that attention on a schedule. They differ in three ways: who operates the machine, whether spare parts are included, and how the work is paid for. Choose the combination that matches your in-house skills, your budget model and how critical the machine is to fleet availability.

Which contract is right for us?

An AMC gives you scheduled preventive visits and breakdown attendance. If you also want spare parts covered within a fixed annual budget, choose a CMC.

An O&M contract lets HYT operate and maintain the machine. If your maintenance department will look after the machine, an Operation Contract covers the operators only.

A call-based Service Contract gives you agreed rates for visits when you need them, and Spare Parts & Support keeps genuine parts within reach.

Yes. Many customers start with an O&M contract while training their own staff, then move to a CMC or AMC. We review the scope with you at each renewal.

Not sure which contract fits?

Tell us about your machine, its utilisation and your in-house team. We will recommend a contract and send a clear scope of work.
