Product overview

What is the CNC Portal Wheel Lathe?

The HYT CNC Portal Wheel Lathe (YPL-02) is purpose-built for the simultaneous reprofiling of new or worn wheels across the complete range of railway vehicle types. Its portal architecture places the cutting heads above the wheelset, allowing roll-through loading of the wheelset.

All load-bearing structures are built from high-strength, close-grained cast iron, delivering exceptional dimensional stability, geometric accuracy and vibration damping – critical for achieving the tight tolerances required in modern railway operations.

Key advantages

Why choose the YPL-02?

Robust portal construction

Heavy-duty portal design ensures superior rigidity and machining stability.

Automatic wheelset handling

An integrated roll-through system increases productivity and reduces handling time.

High-rigidity cast structure

Close-grained cast iron construction delivers exceptional accuracy and vibration damping.

Intelligent wear measurement

Electronic wear compensation maintains optimum cutting depth and consistent wheel profiles.

Enhanced operational safety

Automatic tool retraction protects the machine during power failures and emergency stops.

Operating principle

From worn wheelset to restored profile

  1. 01

    Roll in

    The removed wheelset is rolled into the machine along the shop track.

  2. 02

    Position

    It is raised to the machining position and aligned with the machine axis.

  3. 03

    Clamp & drive

    The wheelset is clamped securely and rotated by the main drive.

  4. 04

    Measure

    Both wheels are measured and the CNC calculates the cut required.

  5. 05

    Rough & finish

    Heavy roughing passes remove defects; a finishing pass restores the exact profile.

  6. 06

    Inspect & roll out

    The result is checked and recorded, and the wheelset leaves for assembly or storage.

Service and support

Engineered into your wheel shop

A portal lathe performs best when wheelsets reach it without waiting. HYT plans the machine's position in the shop together with wheelset tracks, storage and the next process steps, provides the foundation drawing and commissions the lathe on your wheelsets.

For larger facilities we deliver the complete process as a turnkey project or as part of wheel shop automation. Spare supply and maintenance contracts follow for the life of the machine.
