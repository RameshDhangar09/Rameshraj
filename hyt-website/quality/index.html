Credentials

Quality at HYT

A wheel lathe sets the profile that a train runs on. We treat quality as a process that starts with the first drawing and ends only when the machine has proved itself on your wheelsets.

Why it matters

Quality you can measure on the wheel

An error in a machine tool is copied into every part it makes. On a wheel lathe that means every wheel profile, on every train, for years. Accuracy, repeatability and reliability are therefore not extras – they are the product.

Our quality management system is certified to ISO 9001:2015. The certificate confirms that the process exists and is audited; the steps below show how it works in practice. Certificates for quality, environment and occupational health and safety are collected on the Certifications page.

Our quality process

Six checkpoints between order and handover

  1. 01

    Contract & design review

    Customer specifications, wheel profiles, gauge and site conditions are reviewed by engineering before design begins, and each design is checked against them before release.

  2. 02

    Supplier control & incoming inspection

    Castings, structures, bought-out components and CNC systems are sourced from evaluated suppliers and inspected on arrival against drawings and specifications.

  3. 03

    In-process inspection

    Critical machined features, geometry and alignments are checked at defined stages of manufacture and assembly, with results recorded.

  4. 04

    Factory testing

    Each machine is run, its functions and safety interlocks are tested and its geometric accuracy is verified in our works before dispatch.

  5. 05

    Site run-off & acceptance

    After installation, the machine is proved on the customer's own wheelsets. Profiles and dimensions are measured and the results are signed off with the customer.

  6. 06

    Documentation & handover

    Inspection records, test reports, manuals, drawings and spare-parts lists are handed over with the machine, supporting audits and future maintenance.

Beyond the checklist

How we keep improving

Feedback from the field

Service reports and customer feedback are reviewed and fed back into design and manufacturing.

Root-cause analysis

Non-conformities are investigated to their cause and corrected so they do not recur.

Skilled people

Inspectors, assemblers and service engineers are trained in the methods and standards that apply to their work.
