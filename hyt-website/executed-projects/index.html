Machines at work in railway workshops

Our projects range from a single machine added to an existing shed to fleets of wheel lathes for a national railway. In every case the measure of success is the same: wheelsets restored accurately, on time, for years after handover.

The projects below are presented with the client's name, location and scope as far as we are able to publish them. For projects that are still in progress, see Projects Under Execution. To discuss a reference installation relevant to your fleet, contact us.

Executed projects

CNC vertical turning lathe – Sanpada EMU car shed

Client
Sanpada EMU car shed
Location
Mumbai, India
Status
Executed

Suburban EMU fleets run intensive timetables, so every wheelset returned to service quickly counts. A CNC vertical turning lathe gives a car shed its own capacity to machine wheels to profile with accuracy and repeatability, rather than depending on external workshops.

Scope of supply

  • Supply of a CNC vertical turning lathe
  • Machine designed and built at HYT's works in Pune
  • In-house wheel machining capacity for the car shed

Equipment

  • CNC Vertical Turning & Boring Machine

Nine CNC underfloor wheel lathes – Indian Railways

Client
Indian Railways
Location
India
Status
Executed

Indian Railways awarded HYT a contract to supply nine CNC underfloor wheel lathes. Underfloor lathes restore wheel profiles while the wheelset stays in the vehicle, so locomotives and coaches return to traffic without bogie removal.

Scope of supply

  • Contract to supply nine CNC underfloor wheel lathes
  • Machines that re-profile both wheels of a wheelset without removing it from the vehicle
  • Typical scope for underfloor lathes: pit drawings, installation, commissioning and training

Equipment

  • CNC Underfloor Wheel Lathe (YUL-08)
