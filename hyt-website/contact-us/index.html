Contact HYT Engineering

Tell us about your fleet, your workshop or the problem you need to solve. An application engineer will come back to you – usually with questions before answers.

How we can help

Talk to the people who build the machines

Enquiries are answered by HYT's own engineering and sales team in Pune – people who know the machines and the railway workshops they work in. Whether you are planning a new depot, replacing an old lathe or need a quotation for a single machine, the more you tell us, the more useful our first reply will be.

Helpful details include your vehicle types and fleet size, wheel diameter range and profiles, track gauge, expected wheelsets per shift and a sketch of your workshop or depot layout.
