About the partner

Why sliding surfaces matter

Wherever a heavy part moves along a guide – a machine slide, a table, a carriage or a ram – the sliding surface decides how smoothly, accurately and quietly it moves, and how long it lasts. Friction, stick-slip and wear in these surfaces translate directly into lost accuracy and extra maintenance.

"Gleittechnik" is German for sliding technology, and it is SKC Gleittechnik's field. HYT Engineering is the company's exclusive distributor in India, combining the partner's products with our own experience as a builder and servicer of heavy machine tools.

Whether you are building new machines, reconditioning worn slideways or upgrading existing equipment, HYT advises on where sliding technology fits and coordinates supply with SKC.
