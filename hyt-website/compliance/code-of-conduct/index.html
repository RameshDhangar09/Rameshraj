Compliance

Code of Conduct

This Code sets out the standards of behaviour that apply to everyone who works for or on behalf of HYT Engineering Company Pvt. Ltd.

Content pending HYT approval: This Code of Conduct is a draft and requires approval by HYT management and legal counsel before publication. Please also confirm the reporting channel for concerns (named contact or dedicated email address).

Draft – pending approval

1. Purpose and scope

HYT Engineering Company Pvt. Ltd. ("HYT") designs, builds and services machines on which railway safety depends. Our customers, many of them public authorities, trust us to act honestly and responsibly. This Code explains what that means in practice.

The Code applies to all directors, managers and employees of HYT, including temporary and contract staff. We also expect agents, consultants, suppliers, subcontractors and other business partners acting on our behalf to follow equivalent standards.

The Code does not cover every situation. Where it is silent, use good judgement, follow the law and ask for advice when in doubt.

2. Acting with integrity

We comply with all laws and regulations that apply to our business in India and in every country where we work. Where local custom and the law differ, we follow the law. Where this Code sets a higher standard than the law, we follow this Code.

  • Be honest and accurate in everything we say about our products, services and capabilities.
  • Keep promises made to customers, or explain early and openly when we cannot.
  • Never falsify records, test results, inspection reports or certificates.

3. Anti-bribery and anti-corruption

HYT does not tolerate bribery or corruption in any form. We do not offer, promise, give, request or accept any payment, gift or advantage intended to improperly influence a business decision – whether the other party is a public official or a private individual.

This applies equally to payments made through agents, consultants, distributors or relatives. Facilitation payments to speed up routine government actions are not permitted.

  • Gifts and hospitality may be given or accepted only if they are modest, infrequent, transparent and not linked to a pending decision, and only in line with our Business Conduct Guidelines.
  • We respect the procurement rules of our customers, including integrity pacts and codes of conduct in public tenders.
  • Agents and intermediaries are appointed only after due diligence, under written contracts and for documented services at fair market fees.
  • All payments are properly authorised and accurately recorded.

4. Conflicts of interest

A conflict of interest arises when personal interests – financial, family or otherwise – could influence, or appear to influence, decisions made on behalf of HYT.

Employees must avoid such conflicts where possible and disclose any actual or potential conflict to their manager in writing. Examples include a financial interest in a supplier, customer or competitor; outside employment that competes with HYT; or involvement in a purchasing decision affecting a relative.

5. Fair competition

We win business on the merits of our machines, our service and our prices. We comply with competition law in every market we serve.

  • We do not agree prices, bids, markets or customers with competitors, and we do not take part in bid rigging.
  • We do not exchange commercially sensitive information with competitors.
  • We obtain information about competitors and tenders only by lawful and ethical means.
  • We describe competitors' products fairly and accurately.

6. Health and safety

Nobody should be harmed by working for HYT or by using our machines. Our occupational health and safety management system is certified to ISO 45001:2018.

  • Follow safety rules, permits and instructions in our works and on customer sites.
  • Use the personal protective equipment required for the task.
  • Stop work that is unsafe and report hazards, incidents and near misses immediately.
  • Never work under the influence of alcohol or drugs.
  • Design machines with the safety of operators and maintenance staff as a primary requirement.

7. Environment

We work to reduce the environmental impact of our operations and products. Our environmental management system is certified to ISO 14001:2015.

  • Comply with environmental laws and permits.
  • Use energy, materials and water efficiently.
  • Handle, store and dispose of oils, coolants, chemicals and waste correctly.
  • Consider energy use, noise and recyclability when designing machines.

8. Confidential information and intellectual property

Our designs, drawings, software, pricing and know-how are valuable assets. Customers and suppliers also entrust us with their confidential information, including drawings, infrastructure data and project documents.

  • Use confidential information only for the purpose for which it was provided.
  • Share it only with people who need it for their work and are authorised to receive it.
  • Respect the intellectual property of others, including software licences.
  • Confidentiality obligations continue after employment with HYT ends.

9. Data protection and information security

We collect and use personal data – of employees, customers, applicants and suppliers – only for legitimate business purposes, keep it secure and retain it no longer than necessary, in line with applicable data protection law and our Privacy Policy.

Everyone must protect company systems and data: use strong passwords, do not share accounts, report suspected security incidents promptly and take care with remote access to machine control systems.

10. Human rights and fair labour

We respect the human rights of our employees and of everyone affected by our business.

  • We do not use child labour, forced labour or bonded labour, and we do not accept them in our supply chain.
  • We treat people with dignity and do not tolerate harassment, bullying or discrimination on any ground, including gender, caste, religion, ethnicity, age, disability or sexual orientation.
  • We pay at least the legally required wages and benefits and respect working-time rules.
  • We respect employees' lawful right to join or not to join associations.

11. Company assets and accurate records

Company equipment, materials, vehicles and funds are to be used for legitimate business purposes and protected from loss, theft and misuse. Books, records and reports must be complete, accurate and prepared in line with applicable accounting standards and law.

12. Raising concerns

Anyone who suspects a breach of this Code, the law or company policy is encouraged to speak up as early as possible. Concerns can be raised with a line manager, the HR department or a member of senior management. Business partners and members of the public may write to HYT Engineering Company Pvt. Ltd., 29/10, 29/10/1, 29/10/2, D-II Block, MIDC Chinchwad, Pune 411019, India, or email info@hytengineering.com, marking the message "Confidential – Code of Conduct".

Every concern will be taken seriously, handled confidentially as far as the law allows and investigated fairly.

13. No retaliation

HYT will not tolerate retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith or cooperates with an investigation, even if the concern turns out to be unfounded. Retaliation is itself a serious breach of this Code.

14. Compliance with this Code

Managers are responsible for making sure their teams understand and follow this Code. Breaches may lead to disciplinary action up to and including dismissal and, for business partners, termination of the relationship. Where the law has been broken, HYT may inform the relevant authorities.

This Code is reviewed periodically and updated when needed. Practical guidance on applying it is provided in our Business Conduct Guidelines, and our commitments on quality, environment, health and safety are set out in the Policy Statement.
