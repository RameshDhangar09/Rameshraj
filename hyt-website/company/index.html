Our story

From workshop lathes to railway wheel technology

HYT began in 1977 as HYT Engineering Corporation, building small drilling machines and lathes for local industry. In 1989 the business was incorporated as HYT Engineering Company Pvt. Ltd. and set itself a more demanding goal: machining the components on which railway safety depends.

That goal led us to the railway wheelset. A worn wheel profile affects ride quality, rail wear, energy use and derailment risk, and restoring it quickly decides how many trains are available each morning. HYT pioneered CNC wheel lathe technology in India and has kept wheel and axle machining at the centre of its work ever since.

Today we supply underfloor, surface and portal wheel lathes, wheel and axle shop machines, depot equipment and complete workshops to railways, metros and industry – and we stay with each machine for its whole working life. Read the milestones on our History page.

What we do

Machines and services for the whole wheelset life cycle

Everything we make answers one question: how do we keep wheelsets safe, round and in service for longer?

Wheel lathes

Underfloor, tandem, surface and portal lathes that re-profile wheels in hours rather than days.

Wheel & axle shop machines

Vertical turning, axle turning, pressing, measuring and balancing for new and overhauled wheelsets.

Turnkey workshops

Complete wheel shops and depot facilities, from layout and civil interface to commissioning.

Lifetime service

Installation, maintenance contracts, spares, upgrades and training for every machine we deliver.

How we work

Design, build, install, support – under one roof

Keeping every stage in-house means one team is accountable from the first drawing to the last service visit. Nothing is lost at a hand-over between companies.

  1. 01

    Understand

    We study your fleet, wheel profiles, gauge, throughput targets and workshop layout before proposing a machine.

  2. 02

    Design

    Our engineers adapt proven machine platforms to your vehicles, foundations and operating practice.

  3. 03

    Build

    Machines are fabricated, assembled and tested in our own works in Pune under ISO 9001 quality control.

  4. 04

    Install

    We supervise foundations, install and align the machine, and prove it on your own wheelsets before handover.

  5. 05

    Support

    Training, maintenance contracts, genuine spares and upgrades keep the machine productive for decades.

Our values

What guides our decisions

Precision

A tenth of a millimetre on a wheel profile matters. We design, build and test to that standard.

Safety first

For the people who run our machines, the people who build them and the passengers who ride on the wheels they cut.

Accountability

One partner, one point of responsibility – and we answer the phone long after the warranty ends.

Long-term thinking

We judge a machine by its output over twenty years, not by its first week in service.

Practical innovation

New technology earns its place when it makes workshops faster, safer or easier to run.

Integrity

Fair dealing with customers, suppliers and public authorities, as set out in our Code of Conduct.

Who we work with

Trusted by railways and metros

Our customers include Indian Railways, Delhi Metro Rail Corporation, National Capital Region Transport Corporation and the State Railway of Thailand. Their workshops depend on wheel lathes and wheel shop machines to keep fleets in service and within safety limits.

We also meet the international rail industry at exhibitions such as Asia Pacific Rail, Middle East Rail, Africa Rail and AusRAIL, where we share what we have learned about wheelset maintenance with operators from many networks.
