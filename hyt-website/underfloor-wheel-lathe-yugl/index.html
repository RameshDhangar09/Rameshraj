About the machine

Same principle, more automation

Like every underfloor lathe, the YUGL is installed in a pit beneath the track. The vehicle is drawn over the machine, the wheelset is lifted off the rails and both wheels are re-profiled while brake gear, traction motors and bogie frames stay in place.

Where the YUL-08 is HYT's established single-axle machine, the YUGL is positioned as the next-generation platform. It is intended for depots where the lathe runs many cycles a day, operators change from shift to shift and machining data needs to flow into maintenance records. Degree of automation, measuring equipment and data interfaces are agreed with each customer.

Comparing the two models? Our application engineers will set out the differences for your fleet, workload and depot systems.

Key advantages

What the YUGL is designed to deliver

Configurable automation

Sequences from positioning to final measurement can be automated to reduce manual steps between cycles.

Machining records

Before-and-after measurements stored for each wheelset, supporting condition-based maintenance.

Consistent across shifts

Guided operation and stored profiles make results less dependent on individual experience.

No dismantling

The wheelset stays in the vehicle, so trains return to service within the same shift.

Both wheels together

Left and right wheels machined at the same time for matched diameters.

Made for busy fleets

Suited to metro and EMU depots where re-profiling is frequent and maintenance slots are short.

Operating principle

An underfloor cycle with fewer manual steps

  1. 01

    Draw in

    The vehicle is drawn over the pit and the selected wheelset stopped above the machine.

  2. 02

    Lift & clamp

    Drive rollers take the wheel load and the axlebox is clamped.

  3. 03

    Measure

    Both wheels are measured and the readings stored against the wheelset.

  4. 04

    Optimise

    The CNC calculates the minimum cut needed to restore the selected profile.

  5. 05

    Machine

    Both wheels are re-profiled at the same time.

  6. 06

    Record & release

    Final measurements are saved to the machining record and the wheelset is lowered to the rails.

Customer applications

Where the YUGL fits

Metro depots

Frequent re-profiling of high-mileage fleets in short night-time or off-peak windows.

EMU and suburban car sheds

Many identical vehicles, where consistent, recorded cycles simplify fleet planning.

Mainline depots

Locomotives and coaches re-profiled without lifting the vehicle or dropping bogies.

For the long term, a Comprehensive Maintenance Contract (CMC) or an operation and maintenance agreement keeps the lathe available when your fleet needs it.
