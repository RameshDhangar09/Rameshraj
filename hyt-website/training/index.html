Services

Training for operators, programmers and maintainers

A machine is only as good as the people who run it. Our training gives your team the skill and confidence to machine accurately, work safely and keep the machine in condition.

Why it matters

Skilled operators save wheel material

Every millimetre removed from a wheel tread shortens the wheel's life. An operator who understands the measuring system, chooses the right profile and recognises when a cut can be reduced protects wheel life and reduces cost per kilometre. An operator who does not can turn a precise machine into an expensive one.

The same applies to maintenance. Teams that know what to check, what normal looks like and how to read the machine's diagnostics catch problems before they become breakdowns.

Our trainers are engineers who build, commission and service HYT machines. Training uses your own machine wherever possible, so your team learns on the equipment they will use every day.

Who we train

Programmes for each role

Operators

Safe positioning, measuring, profile selection, machining cycles and daily care.

CNC programmers

Profile programs, parameters, machining strategies and measurement data.

Mechanical maintainers

Lubrication, hydraulics, adjustments and replacement of wear parts.

Electrical maintainers

Drives, sensors, interlocks, diagnostics and fault-finding.

Typical training topics

Content is tailored to your machine and your team's experience. A typical programme covers:

  • Machine layout, safety zones, guards and emergency procedures
  • Positioning vehicles or wheelsets and clamping correctly
  • Wheel profile measurement and interpreting the results
  • Selecting profiles and minimising material removal
  • Running machining cycles and checking the finished profile
  • Daily, weekly and periodic maintenance tasks
  • Hydraulic, pneumatic and lubrication systems
  • Reading alarms and using the CNC diagnostics
  • Replacing common wear parts and cutting inserts
  • Keeping machining and maintenance records

Training formats

  • Initial training during installation and commissioning
  • On-site courses on your own machine
  • Sessions at the HYT works in Pune, on request
  • Refresher courses for experienced staff
  • Induction training for new team members
  • Training on new functions after a retro-fitment

Process

How we plan a training programme

  1. 01

    Needs review

    We agree the roles, experience levels and objectives with you.

  2. 02

    Programme

    Theory and hands-on sessions are scheduled around your workshop programme.

  3. 03

    Delivery

    Training takes place on your machine wherever possible, with printed reference material.

  4. 04

    Assessment

    Participants demonstrate the key tasks, and attendance is recorded.
