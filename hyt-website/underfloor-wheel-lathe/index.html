About the machine

Wheel maintenance without taking the train apart

Flats, hollow treads and thin flanges are a fact of railway life. The traditional remedy – dropping the bogie, pulling the wheelset and sending it to the wheel shop – costs days of availability and a great deal of labour. The YUL-08 brings the machine to the wheelset instead.

Installed in a pit beneath a straight section of track, the lathe lifts the axle off the rails on its own drive rollers, clamps the axlebox securely and machines both wheels at the same time. Brake gear, traction motors and bogie frames stay where they are. When the cycle ends, the wheelset is lowered back on to the rails and the next axle is positioned over the machine.

Because the vehicle stays coupled and assembled, one YUL-08 can keep a large fleet within profile limits and shorten the time each train spends out of service.

Key advantages

Why workshops choose an underfloor lathe

Short turnaround

No bogie drop or wheelset removal, so vehicles return to traffic within the same shift.

Both wheels at once

Left and right wheels are machined simultaneously, matching diameters within the required tolerance.

Minimum material removal

Automatic measurement before cutting lets the CNC take only the depth needed to restore the profile.

One machine, mixed fleet

Locomotives, coaches, EMUs and wagons handled with suitable adaptors and stored profile programs.

Safe operation

Guarded working zone, interlocks and clear operator controls at track level.

Built for service

Standard industrial components and HYT spare parts support keep downtime low.

Operating principle

From arrival to departure in six steps

  1. 01

    Position

    The vehicle is drawn over the pit and the selected wheelset is stopped above the machine.

  2. 02

    Lift & clamp

    Drive rollers rise to take the wheel load; the axlebox is clamped to hold the wheelset steady.

  3. 03

    Measure

    Probes record diameter, flange height and flange thickness on both wheels.

  4. 04

    Calculate

    The CNC compares the readings with the target profile and determines the minimum cut.

  5. 05

    Machine

    Both wheels are profiled simultaneously while the rollers rotate the wheelset.

  6. 06

    Verify & release

    A final measurement confirms the result, a report is generated and the wheelset is lowered to the rails.

Main features

  • Rigid, stress-relieved machine bed designed for pit installation
  • Four independently driven friction rollers for reliable wheel rotation
  • Hydraulic axlebox clamping adaptable to different bogie designs
  • Two CNC-controlled tool slides, one per wheel
  • Automatic chip handling from the cutting zone
  • Profile library and machining reports stored in the CNC
  • Operator panel positioned for a clear view of the work area
  • Provision for brake-disc machining on suitable configurations

Service and support

Supported from foundation drawing to final overhaul

An underfloor lathe is part of the building. HYT supplies pit and foundation drawings early in the project, supervises installation and proves the machine on your own wheelsets before handover. Operators and maintenance staff are trained on site.

After commissioning, choose the level of support that suits your depot – from on-call service to an Annual Maintenance Contract or a Comprehensive Maintenance Contract with spares included.
