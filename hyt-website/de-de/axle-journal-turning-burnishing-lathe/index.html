About the machine

Journal finishing, not full axle turning

Every wheelset overhaul brings the axle journals under scrutiny. Corrosion, fretting marks and wear at the bearing seats must be removed, and the journal returned to a diameter and surface finish that the bearing specification accepts.

The CNC Axle Journal Turning & Burnishing Lathe is built for exactly that task. It turns the journal and adjacent seats to size, then burnishes the surface: hardened rollers press down the peaks left by turning and leave a smooth, compacted finish. Burnishing is a cold-working process – it produces no chips.

This is an overhaul machine. For turning complete axles from raw or forged blanks in new production, see the CNC Axle Turning Lathe ATL-01.

Key advantages

Why turn and burnish on one machine

Correct bearing fit

Journals restored to the diameter required for a reliable bearing fit.

Smooth, compacted finish

Burnishing leaves a fine surface without removing further material.

More durable surface

A burnished surface resists wear and fretting better than a turned surface alone.

Longer axle life

Removing only the damaged layer keeps axles within limits for more overhauls.

No transfer between machines

Turning and burnishing on the same machine saves handling and set-up time.

Repeatable programs

Stored programs for each axle type keep results consistent.

Machining process

Restoring a journal in six steps

  1. 01

    Load

    The axle or wheelset is lifted into the machine.

  2. 02

    Centre & clamp

    It is aligned on the machine axis and secured.

  3. 03

    Measure

    Journal diameters and damage are assessed against overhaul limits.

  4. 04

    Turn

    The journal is turned to the next permissible size, removing corrosion and wear marks.

  5. 05

    Burnish

    Hardened rollers smooth and compact the turned surface.

  6. 06

    Inspect

    Diameter and finish are checked before the axle moves on to bearing fitting.

Metro maintenance

Service and support

Consistent journals, overhaul after overhaul

HYT sets up the machine for your axle types, proves the turning and burnishing parameters during commissioning and trains your operators on overhaul limits and inspection.

Burnishing tools, wear parts and other consumables are available through spare supply, and maintenance contracts keep the machine accurate.
