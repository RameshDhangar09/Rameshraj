About the machine

Decisions start with measurement

Should this wheelset be re-profiled, re-wheeled or scrapped? Is the new wheelset within tolerance before it leaves the shop? The answers depend on measurements – and manual gauges are slow, operator-dependent and hard to document.

A wheel measuring station records the key geometry of wheels and wheelsets in a repeatable cycle: tread diameters, flange height and thickness, back-to-back distance and further parameters specified by the operator. Results are stored and compared with your limits.

Placed before a wheel lathe, the station helps plan the cut; placed after machining or pressing, it confirms the result. In an automated wheel shop it becomes the data point that tells each wheelset where to go next.

Key advantages

Why measure automatically

Repeatable results

The same cycle for every wheelset, independent of who operates the station.

Faster inspection

Several dimensions captured in one cycle instead of one gauge at a time.

Digital records

Results stored per wheelset for traceability and trend analysis.

Clear decisions

Automatic comparison with limits shows whether a wheelset passes.

Better re-profiling plans

Accurate data before machining helps minimise material removal.

Ready for integration

Can share data with lathes, presses and shop systems where interfaces are agreed.

Operating principle

One cycle, a complete record

  1. 01

    Roll in

    The wheelset arrives at the station on the shop track or from handling equipment.

  2. 02

    Locate

    It is positioned and held in the measuring position.

  3. 03

    Measure

    The specified dimensions are captured on both wheels.

  4. 04

    Compare

    Results are checked against your limits and tolerances.

  5. 05

    Record

    Data is stored against the wheelset's identity.

  6. 06

    Route

    The wheelset goes on to machining, pressing, dispatch or rejection.

Measurements you can trust

A measuring station is only as good as its calibration. HYT commissions the station against reference standards, trains your inspectors and includes routine verification in maintenance contracts.

For new facilities we plan the station as part of the complete material and data flow – see Wheel Shop Automation.
