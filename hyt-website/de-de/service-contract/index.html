Maintenance Services

Service Contract: call-based support at agreed rates

Pay for engineer visits when you need them, at rates agreed in advance. A Service Contract gives you access to HYT expertise without committing to a fixed annual fee.

Inhalt noch nicht von HYT freigegeben: This service overlaps with AMC and CMC. HYT to confirm that the Service Contract should be offered as a call-based, pay-per-visit agreement and confirm its scope.

How it works

Flexible support without an annual commitment

Not every machine needs a full maintenance contract. A lathe that runs a few shifts a month, or a workshop with an experienced in-house maintenance team, may only need specialist help occasionally. A Service Contract sets the commercial terms in advance so that, when you do need us, nothing is held up by negotiation or purchase approvals.

We agree a rate card with you – engineer day rates, travel and any standard charges – for an agreed period. When you raise a call, we assign an engineer, attend, diagnose and repair, and invoice for that visit only. Preventive visits can be booked on the same terms whenever you choose.

If your call volume grows, an Annual Maintenance Contract or CMC will usually work out more economical and gives you scheduled preventive care. We will tell you when that point is reached.

Process

How a service call works

  1. 01

    Agree terms

    Rates, travel charges and the contract period are fixed in advance.

  2. 02

    Raise a call

    Report the fault or request a visit through the toll-free line or by email, quoting the machine serial number.

  3. 03

    Initial diagnosis

    Our service desk discusses symptoms with your team to prepare the right engineer and parts.

  4. 04

    Site visit

    The engineer attends, diagnoses and repairs, and agrees any parts needed with you.

  5. 05

    Report and invoice

    You receive a visit report and an invoice for that visit at the agreed rates.

Included and not included

  • Included: engineer attendance on each call at agreed rates
  • Included: diagnosis and repair labour during the visit
  • Included: preventive visits when you book them
  • Included: a written report for every visit
  • Not included: spare parts – quoted separately
  • Not included: a fixed number of scheduled visits per year
  • Not included: machine operation
  • Not included: damage from accidents, misuse or supply faults
