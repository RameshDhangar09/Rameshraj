Solutions for Rolling Stock Manufacturers

New vehicles start with new wheelsets. HYT supplies the machines that turn, bore, press, measure and balance them – individually or as a complete, traceable wheel shop.

The challenge

Tight tolerances at production volumes

A rolling stock manufacturer must deliver wheelsets that meet the customer's specification first time, at the rate the assembly line demands. Bores must suit the press fit, journals must be finished for reliable bearing seats, and every wheelset must be measured, balanced and documented before it reaches the bogie.

Production programmes change: a new metro order, a different gauge, a ramp-up for an export contract. The machines in the wheel shop must adapt without long changeovers, and the data they produce must stand up to customer inspection and later warranty questions.

How HYT helps

A complete process from raw component to certified wheelset

Wheel turning and boring

CNC vertical turning and boring machines finish wheels and bores to tight tolerances.

Axle machining

Axle turning and journal burnishing for accurate, durable bearing and wheel seats.

Pressing with records

Wheel presses for mounting and dismounting wheels on axles.

Measurement

Wheel measuring stations confirm geometry before dispatch.

Balancing

Dynamic balancing for smooth running at higher speeds.

Automation and flow

Linked machines, handling and data in an automated wheel shop.
