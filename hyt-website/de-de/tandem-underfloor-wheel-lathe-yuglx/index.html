About the machine

A full bogie per cycle, with more automation

In a busy depot the wheel lathe is often the bottleneck. A tandem underfloor lathe addresses that by machining both wheelsets of a bogie together, with the vehicle still assembled above the pit.

The YUGLX is positioned as the next-generation tandem platform alongside the established YUL-12T. It is intended for fleets where the machine is in near-continuous use, and where consistent cycles and complete machining records matter as much as cutting time. Automation scope, measuring equipment and data interfaces are agreed with each customer.

Its single-axle counterpart is the YUGL.

Key advantages

What the YUGLX is designed to deliver

Whole bogie per setting

Two wheelsets machined together, with no vehicle move between axles.

Configurable automation

Steps from positioning to final measurement can be automated to suit the depot.

Complete records

Measurements for all four wheels stored per bogie for trend analysis and maintenance planning.

Wheelbase flexibility

Machine spacing configured to the bogie wheelbases in your fleet.

Less operator dependence

Guided, repeatable cycles give consistent results across shifts.

Safe pit operation

Guarded working zones and interlocks around both machines.

Operating principle

Automated tandem cycle

  1. 01

    Draw in

    The vehicle is drawn over the pit and the bogie stopped above the machines.

  2. 02

    Align

    The machines are aligned with both wheelsets of the bogie.

  3. 03

    Lift & clamp

    Drive rollers take the wheel load and the axleboxes are clamped.

  4. 04

    Measure

    All four wheels are measured and the readings stored against the bogie.

  5. 05

    Machine

    Both wheelsets are re-profiled in synchronised operation.

  6. 06

    Record & release

    Results are saved, the wheelsets lowered and the vehicle moved to the next bogie.

Main features

  • Two pit-mounted machines operating as a synchronised pair
  • Both wheelsets of a bogie machined in one setting
  • Automatic measurement of all four wheels
  • Automation scope agreed per project
  • Machining records per bogie and wheelset
  • Profile library for mixed fleets
  • Interlocked guarding around both machines
  • Pit and foundation drawings supplied by HYT

Customer applications

Where the YUGLX fits

Metro depots

Large, uniform fleets where the lathe runs almost continuously.

Regional and suburban fleets

Multiple units with tight availability targets and short maintenance windows.

Data-driven maintenance

Operators who plan re-profiling from measured wear trends rather than fixed intervals.

Service and support

From pit design to data integration

A tandem lathe is a long-term investment in depot capacity. HYT plans the pit and rail interface with your civil contractor, agrees automation and data scope with your maintenance and IT teams, and commissions both machines on your own trains.

Afterwards, operation and maintenance services or a Comprehensive Maintenance Contract (CMC) keep availability high, and retro-fitment keeps the control technology current.
