Maintenance Services

Comprehensive Maintenance Contract (CMC)

Preventive maintenance, breakdown attendance and the spare parts to fix the machine – all within one annual fee. A CMC turns maintenance from an unpredictable cost into a fixed budget line.

How it works

Maintenance and spares under one fixed fee

A CMC includes everything in an Annual Maintenance Contract – scheduled preventive visits and breakdown attendance – and adds the spare parts needed to keep the machine running. When a covered part fails or reaches the end of its life, HYT supplies and fits it without a separate purchase order.

That matters in organisations where procurement of a single part can take longer than the repair itself. With a CMC, the machine is back in service as soon as the part arrives, and your annual maintenance cost is known in advance.

Because HYT carries the cost of parts, we have every reason to maintain the machine thoroughly and to replace wear parts before they cause damage elsewhere. Your operators continue to run the machine; we take care of its condition.

Included

What a CMC includes

Scheduled preventive visits

An agreed visit plan with inspection, adjustment and servicing to the HYT checklist.

Breakdown attendance

Engineers diagnose and rectify faults reported during the contract period.

Spare parts

Mechanical, hydraulic, electrical and electronic parts as listed in the contract, supplied and fitted.

Accuracy checks

Measuring system and machining results verified and adjusted where required.

Reports and history

Visit reports and a parts history that support budgeting and long-term planning.

Fixed annual fee

One budget line for labour and listed spares, agreed before the contract starts.

What is not included

Unless specifically listed in your contract, the following are outside the CMC scope:

  • Consumables such as cutting inserts, lubricants, hydraulic oil and filters
  • Day-to-day operation of the machine
  • Parts damaged by accidents, misuse, fire, flooding or power supply faults
  • Civil, structural and track work around the machine
  • Retro-fitment, upgrades and modifications
  • Parts not listed in the contract schedule
