About the plant

Clean trains without the manual effort

A clean train matters to passengers, to the driver's view and to the condition of paint, windows and seals. Washing by hand is slow, physically demanding and hard to do consistently across a long fleet.

An automated washing plant is installed on a depot track. The train runs through at low speed while the plant wets the body, applies detergent, scrubs with rotating brushes and rinses. Sensors detect the vehicle and control each stage, so water and detergent are used only where a train is present.

Plant layout, brush arrangement, chemicals and water handling are designed around your vehicle profiles, depot track and local water conditions.

Quick passage

Profile-matched brushes

Brush arrangement designed around your vehicle profiles.

Process

What happens as a train passes through

  1. 01

    Detect

    Sensors register the approaching train and start the sequence.

  2. 02

    Pre-wet

    The body is wetted to loosen dirt.

  3. 03

    Apply detergent

    Cleaning agent is applied at the set concentration.

  4. 04

    Brush

    Rotating brushes scrub sides and other specified surfaces.

  5. 05

    Rinse

    Clean water removes detergent and loosened dirt.

  6. 06

    Collect

    Wash water is drained to collection or recovery as designed.

Service and support

Designed, built and maintained for your depot

HYT reviews your fleet, track layout and water supply before designing the plant, then installs, commissions and hands it over with trained operators.

Brushes, nozzles and pumps wear in normal use. An Annual Maintenance Contract and spare supply keep the plant washing to the same standard year after year.
