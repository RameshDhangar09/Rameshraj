About HYT

Our History

Almost five decades of precision engineering in Pune – from the first machines for Indian Railways in 1977 to an international group with SAFOP S.p.A. in Italy.

The HYT story

From drilling machines to a global engineering group

Established in 1977 under the leadership of Mr. Bhojraj Hemraj Teli, HYT Engineering Company began with a mission to manufacture quality precision machines. From beginning as a maker of drilling machines, HYT has grown into a trusted engineering group, providing advanced railway maintenance machinery, precision machine tools and turnkey solutions to customers in India and international markets.

Era · 1970s

Foundation

  1. 1977

    Founding milestone

    Establishment of HYT Engineering Corporation

    Mr. Bhojraj Hemraj Teli established HYT Engineering Corporation in Pune with a single mission – to manufacture quality precision machines for Railways. Early work focused on machines for the rehabilitation of traction motors and the regeneration of armatures, laying the foundation for a specialised engineering enterprise.

Era · 1980s

Early innovation

  1. 1981

    First development

    Development and supply of the Mica Under Cutting Machine

    HYT developed and supplied the Mica Under Cutting Machine – an early specialised product showing the company's growing capability in precision machine tool development for the railway sector.

  2. 1985

    Product innovation

    Development and supply of the Universal Armature Machine

    The Universal Armature Machine was developed and supplied, expanding HYT's portfolio of specialised machines for railway traction motor maintenance and rehabilitation.

  3. 1989

    Incorporation

    HYT Engineering Company Pvt. Ltd.

    The business was incorporated as HYT Engineering Company Pvt. Ltd., giving it the structure to take on larger engineering contracts.

Era · 1990s

Wheel lathe pioneer

  1. 1992

    India first

    India's first indigenous Surface Wheel Lathe

    A landmark year. HYT developed and supplied India's first indigenous Surface Wheel Lathe, marking the company's entry into wheel lathe technology.

  2. 1994

    India first

    India's first indigenous Underfloor Wheel Lathe and Axle Journal Turning Lathe

    HYT achieved another national first, developing and supplying India's first indigenous Underfloor (Pit) Wheel Lathe and the Axle Journal Turning and Burnishing Lathe.

Era · 2000s

The CNC era and global reach

  1. 2001

    Group expansion

    Establishment of HYT Innovative Projects Pvt. Ltd.

    HYT expanded the group by establishing HYT Innovative Projects Pvt. Ltd. as a sister concern, specialising in precision tube manufacture using Flow Forming technology, from a 4,500 m² facility with a team of 50+ employees.

  2. 2001

    Technology upgrade

    From non-CNC to CNC machines

    HYT completed a transformative upgrade from non-CNC to CNC, developing and supplying the CNC Surface Wheel Lathe and the CNC Vertical Turning Lathe – positioning itself as a technology-led engineering company aligned with global manufacturing standards.

  3. 2004

    HYT goes global

    Export order – SMH Rail Sdn. Bhd., Malaysia

    A defining milestone. HYT received an international order from SMH Rail Sdn. Bhd., Malaysia, beginning the company's global footprint and its transformation into an internationally recognised machine tool exporter.

  4. 2005

    Strategic project

    Reconditioning of a submarine manufacturing machine – Mazagon Dock Ltd.

    HYT demonstrated its versatility by successfully reconditioning a submarine manufacturing machine for Mazagon Dock Ltd., extending its expertise beyond railways into defence and heavy engineering.

  5. 2007

    Major order

    Larsen & Toubro order

    HYT received a landmark order from Larsen & Toubro for the supply of machines for wheel disc manufacturing – one of its largest single orders, cementing HYT's position.

  6. 2009

    India first

    Automated Train Wash Plant – Kurla, Mumbai

    HYT developed and supplied India's first Automated Train Wash Plant for Indian Railways at Kurla, Mumbai – introducing a new product category and demonstrating the ability to innovate beyond wheel lathes into broader rail infrastructure.

Era · 2010s

Turnkey leadership and global expansion

  1. 2010

    Technology upgrade

    CNC Vertical Turning and Boring Machine

    HYT developed and supplied the CNC Vertical Turning and Boring Machine – a high-precision machine for large-diameter components that significantly expanded the company's addressable market and technical capability.

  2. 2011

    First EPC project

    LHB coach shell manufacturing facility – ICF Chennai

    HYT entered the turnkey EPC domain with its first major infrastructure project, setting up an LHB coach shell manufacturing facility for Integral Coach Factory, Chennai. This marked the transformation from machine tool supplier to complete infrastructure solutions provider.

  3. 2013

    Record order

    Largest-ever single order for CNC Surface Wheel Lathes – 36 machines

    HYT received its largest single order for CNC Surface Wheel Lathes, for 36 machines, demonstrating the scale of demand for the company's flagship products across Indian Railways.

  4. 2013

    Leadership

    Ms. Rajashri Teli appointed Whole Time Director

    Ms. Rajashri Bhojraj Teli was appointed Whole Time Director, bringing progressive business strategies that would drive significant expansion and global recognition for the group.

  5. 2014

    Product milestone

    CNC Portal Wheel Lathe – North Central Railway, Jhansi

    HYT supplied the CNC Portal Wheel Lathe to North Central Railway's wagon repair workshop at Jhansi, adding another variant to its wheel lathe portfolio and strengthening its relationship with Indian Railways.

  6. 2015

    Product milestone

    CNC Underfloor Wheel Lathes – Chennai Metro

    HYT expanded into the metro segment by supplying CNC Underfloor Wheel Lathes to Chennai Metro – the first of many metro contracts that established HYT as a preferred supplier for India's growing urban rail network.

  7. 2015

    Make in India

    Rail cum Road Vehicle (Shunter) developed with BEMO, Netherlands

    HYT developed the Rail cum Road Vehicle (Shunter) under a Make in India initiative in collaboration with BEMO, Netherlands – a versatile vehicle for railway yard operations.

  8. 2015

    Infrastructure

    Green Building headquarters established in Pune

    HYT established its Green Building headquarters in Chinchwad, Pune – a modern, sustainable facility that became the central hub for engineering, manufacturing and administration.

  9. 2016

    Technology transfer

    CNC spring coiling machines – technology transfer from TNI Spring Technologies, USA

    HYT manufactured and supplied the CNC Hot Helical Spring Coiling Machine, CNC Hot Bar End Tapering Machine and Re-pitching Machine for the spring project at ICF Chennai, under a technology transfer from TNI Spring Technologies LLC, USA – demonstrating HYT's ability to assimilate and deploy international technology.

  10. 2018

    OEM supply

    Supply to Alstom and GE Diesel Locomotive Pvt. Ltd.

    HYT supplied new-generation Underfloor Wheel Lathe machines to Alstom and GE Diesel Locomotive Pvt. Ltd., marking entry into the international OEM supply chain for rolling stock manufacturers.

  11. 2018

    Service model

    Wet leasing (Machine as a Service) – ICF Chennai

    HYT pioneered a wet leasing contract for ICF Chennai, taking full responsibility for supply, commissioning, operation and maintenance – a first-of-its-kind service model in the Indian rail machinery sector. See wet leasing.

  12. 2019

    SAFOP acquisition

    Acquisition of SAFOP S.p.A., Italy – 100% subsidiary

    HYT's most significant strategic move – the acquisition of SAFOP S.p.A., Italy (established 1923) as a subsidiary. With over 1,400 machines sold globally and a century of Italian precision engineering, SAFOP dramatically expanded the group's global presence and technology portfolio.

  13. 2019

    National first

    India's first indigenous automated wheel and axle assembly plant – RWF Bangalore

    HYT developed and set up India's first indigenous Automated Wheel and Axle Assembly Plant for Rail Wheel Factory, Bangalore – a landmark in railway wheelset manufacturing automation.

  14. 2019

    Export milestone

    Supply to SNCF France – compliant with European norms

    HYT developed and supplied Underfloor Wheel Lathes to SNCF France in compliance with European standards – a landmark export achievement that validated HYT's quality on the world stage.

Era · 2020s

New horizons

  1. 2020

    Record order

    Order for 27 Underfloor Wheel Lathes – Indian Railways

    HYT received an order for 27 Underfloor Wheel Lathes from Indian Railways – one of the largest single wheel lathe orders, reinforcing its position as the preferred supplier for Indian Railways' rolling stock maintenance infrastructure.

  2. 2021

    Africa

    CNC Underfloor Wheel Lathe supplied to Gabon, and CNC Surface Wheel Lathe to Ghana

    HYT supplied and commissioned a CNC Underfloor Wheel Lathe in Gabon, Central Africa, setting up a complete wheel shop in the region, and delivered a CNC Surface Wheel Lathe to Ghana, West Africa – demonstrating strong demand across the African rail market.

  3. 2021

    EPC projects

    Automated wheel shop at New Katni and axle machining line at RWF Bangalore

    HYT executed an automated wheel shop EPC project at New Katni, Madhya Pradesh – one of Indian Railways' largest wheel maintenance facilities – and set up a complete axle machining line at Rail Wheel Factory, Bangalore.

  4. 2021

    Product innovation

    India's first indigenous Tandem Underfloor Wheel Lathe

    HYT developed India's first indigenous Tandem Underfloor Wheel Lathe – a twin-machine configuration for ultra-high-throughput wheel shops, awarded by Delhi Metro Rail Corporation.

  5. 2022

    National first

    First Vande Bharat maintenance depot – Shakur Basti, Northern Railway

    HYT set up India's first dedicated maintenance depot for the Vande Bharat Express at Shakur Basti under Northern Railway – a landmark achievement as the country's flagship semi-high-speed train entered service, with HYT machines integral to its upkeep.

  6. 2024

    Product innovation

    Bogie Assembly and Disassembly Line – Dibrugarh

    HYT delivered a Bogie Assembly and Disassembly Line at Dibrugarh for Northeast Frontier Railway – a sophisticated semi-automated assembly solution bringing modern manufacturing practice to one of India's most geographically challenging rail regions.

  7. 2024

    Product innovation

    Large horizontal machines for turbine rotor maintenance

    HYT and SAFOP secured orders for large horizontal machines for the maintenance of turbine rotors.

  8. 2025

    Global presence

    Five international exhibitions across four continents

    HYT exhibited at Middle East Rail in Dubai, Eurasia Rail in Istanbul, Asia Pacific Rail in Bangkok, Africa Rail in Johannesburg and NT Expo in São Paulo – the company's widest international showing in a single year.

  9. 2025

    Global presence

    Orders from the Middle East and Turkey

    HYT secured orders from the Middle East and Turkey for underfloor wheel lathes, tandem underfloor wheel lathes, axle turning lathes and vertical turning lathes.

  10. 2026

    Recognition

    Managing Director recognised at Hurun Stars of the Deccan

    Ms. Rajashri Teli, Managing Director, was recognised at Hurun Stars of the Deccan 2026 – acknowledging her role in the group's growth and international expansion.

  11. 2026

    InnoTrans 2026

    HYT Group exhibits at InnoTrans 2026, Berlin

    HYT Engineering and SAFOP S.p.A. exhibited together as the HYT Group at InnoTrans 2026 – Hall 22A, Stands 630 & 730, Messe Berlin, 22–25 September 2026 – presenting the full CNC wheel lathe range to the world's leading transport technology audience.
