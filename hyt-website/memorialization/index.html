Work With Us

Life at HYT

Our machines are built by people. These are some of the moments we share as a team – at work, for our health and safety, and with our community.

Our people

More than a workplace

Precision machines need people who care about details – and people care more when they feel part of a team. Throughout the year, colleagues from design, the shop floor, service and the office come together for events that have nothing to do with deadlines.

Our annual meet brings everyone together to celebrate the year's work. Safety week reminds us that every machine we build is built safely. Blood donation camps and our community initiatives let us give something back, and International Yoga Day is a chance to look after ourselves.

Through the year

What we do together

Annual meet

The whole team in one place to look back on the year and celebrate the work of colleagues.

Safety week

Talks, drills and shop-floor activities that keep safe working habits fresh for everyone.

Blood donation camp

Colleagues volunteer to donate blood for patients who need it.

International Yoga Day

A shared session on 21 June that puts health and well-being first.
