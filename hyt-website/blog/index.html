HYT Blog

Articles, viewpoints and stories from the people who design, build and service HYT machines.

Blog or Knowledge Centre?

Two kinds of reading

The Blog is where we publish dated articles: opinions on maintenance practice, lessons from commissioning sites, stories from our projects and reflections on where railway workshops are heading. Each post reflects its moment and carries a publication date.

The Knowledge Centre holds lasting technical guides and answers to frequently asked questions – material we keep up to date and that you can return to when planning a lathe, a pit or a re-profiling programme.

If you are looking for company announcements such as orders and exhibitions, see Latest News.
