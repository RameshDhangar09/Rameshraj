Maintenance Services

Annual Maintenance Contract (AMC)

Planned preventive visits and breakdown attendance from engineers who know your machine, for a fixed annual fee. Your team operates the machine; we keep it in good health.

How it works

Predictable labour cost, planned machine care

An AMC is the most common way to keep an HYT machine in condition after the warranty period. Your operators continue to run the machine every day. HYT engineers visit at agreed intervals to inspect, adjust and service it, and attend when something goes wrong.

The fee covers our engineers' time for the agreed number of preventive visits and for breakdown attendance during the contract year. Spare parts are not included: when a part is needed, we tell you which one, why and what it costs, and supply it against your order. If you would rather have spares within a fixed budget too, look at the Comprehensive Maintenance Contract (CMC).

Regular visits catch wear before it causes a breakdown, keep machining accuracy within limits and build a service history that helps when planning upgrades or replacement.

Included

What an AMC includes

Scheduled preventive visits

An agreed number of visits per year, planned around your workshop programme.

Inspection checklists

Mechanical, hydraulic, pneumatic, electrical and CNC checks to a standard HYT checklist.

Accuracy checks

Measuring system and machining results verified, with adjustments made where required.

Breakdown attendance

Engineers attend breakdowns reported during the contract year to diagnose and rectify faults.

Visit reports

A written report after every visit, listing findings, work done and open points.

Spares recommendations

Advance notice of parts approaching the end of their life, so you can order before they fail.

What is not included

Unless specifically listed in your contract, the following are outside the AMC scope:

  • Spare parts – quoted and supplied separately against your order
  • Consumables such as cutting inserts, lubricants, hydraulic oil and filters
  • Day-to-day operation of the machine
  • Damage caused by accidents, misuse, fire, flooding or power supply faults
  • Civil, structural and track work around the machine
  • Retro-fitment, upgrades and modifications

The contract year

What a typical AMC year looks like

  1. 01

    Start-up inspection

    We record the machine's condition at the start of the contract and flag any work needed.

  2. 02

    Visit plan

    Preventive visit dates are agreed in advance to fit your workshop programme.

  3. 03

    Preventive visits

    Checks, adjustments and servicing are carried out and documented.

  4. 04

    Breakdown calls

    You report a fault through the service desk; an engineer is assigned and attends.

  5. 05

    Annual review

    Machine history and recommendations are discussed before renewal.
