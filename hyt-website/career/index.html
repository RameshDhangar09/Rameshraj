Our culture

Where ambitions are realised

HYT is more than a workplace – it is a platform where engineers, managers and specialists grow into leaders. With a PAN India presence and a growing global footprint, every role at HYT connects to real railway infrastructure that moves millions of people every day.

  1. 01

    Innovation-driven

    Regular challenges that take you beyond your comfort zone, in an environment where your best work is noticed and put to use.

  2. 02

    Ownership & growth

    Real ownership of projects – from the shop floor to installation at the customer's site – building accountability and expertise early in your career.

  3. 03

    Global exposure

    Work on railway projects across India and in international markets, and gain rare domain expertise in precision railway machinery.

Employee benefits

A range of benefits for you and your family

We look after the people who build our machines – at work, at home and on the way in between.

Medical benefits

Comprehensive medical cover for employees and their families, including emergency healthcare support and wellness programmes.

Employee-friendly environment

An open, collaborative culture that values respect, diversity and team-first thinking across all departments and levels.

Loan facility

Employee loans at favourable interest rates for personal, medical or educational needs – because we invest in your stability.

Learning & development

Structured technical and professional training, mentoring and on-the-job learning alongside experienced specialists.

Canteen facility

A subsidised in-house canteen serving nutritious meals, so your working day is well fuelled from morning to evening.

Cab facility

Company-arranged cab services for eligible employees, for a safer and easier daily commute.

Facilities & workplace

Where you will work

From design desks to assembly bays and customer sites, these are the places where HYT careers take shape.

Pune campus & offices

Our Pune campus brings management, design, purchasing, sales and support teams together in a green, landscaped setting close to the manufacturing works.

  • Modern offices and meeting rooms
  • Easy access to the shop floor and test areas
  • Landscaped, well-kept surroundings

Manufacturing & assembly

Machines are built, wired and tested in our own halls. Assembly teams work alongside designers, so ideas move quickly from drawing to machine.

  • Large machine assembly bays with overhead cranes
  • Machining, fitting and electrical assembly
  • Factory testing before dispatch

Engineering & design

Mechanical, electrical and CNC engineers design every HYT machine in-house – from concept and calculations to drawings, programs and documentation.

  • 3D design and simulation
  • Electrical, hydraulic and CNC engineering
  • Direct contact with customers' engineers

Quality & inspection

Our quality team checks components and complete machines against drawings, standards and customer specifications, in line with ISO 9001:2015.

  • Incoming and in-process inspection
  • Run-off trials on real wheelsets
  • ISO 9001, 14001 and 45001 systems

Training & learning

New colleagues learn the machines, safety rules and customer requirements through structured training, mentoring and time on real projects.

  • Induction and safety training
  • Technical and soft-skill programmes
  • Mentoring by experienced specialists

Canteen & commute

A subsidised canteen and company-arranged cabs for eligible employees take care of the everyday – so you can focus on your work.

  • Nutritious, subsidised meals
  • Company cab services for eligible employees
  • Celebrations, safety weeks and wellness events
